Meta's Edits app launches iPhone Duo version and tests desktop editor

Social News · 25 October 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

Meta has released a dedicated version of Edits, its short-form video editing app, built for Apple's folding iPhone Duo. The interface adapts to the device's larger screen, orientation and crease, letting users edit and preview their timeline side by side. Separately, a desktop version first announced in June is now in live testing with selected creators, according to a screenshot shared by creator Taylor Golub.

The desktop layout shows more editing elements on one screen, making it easier to move between the app's creation tools. Instagram chief Adam Mosseri commented that he sees the desktop version becoming a base for many future features, signalling continued investment in Edits as Meta's core creation tool for Reels.

For sports social teams, a desktop Edits could bridge the gap between quick phone edits on matchday and longer post-production sessions in the office, keeping Reels workflows inside Meta's ecosystem. Clubs and athletes already cutting highlights, player features or sponsor clips in Edits should watch for wider desktop access, while staff using folding phones gain a bigger mobile canvas for fast turnaround content from the stadium.

Instagram tests Features, horizontal videos up to 90 minutes, to rival YouTube

Instagram is testing a format called Features that lets creators upload horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes, aimed at podcasts, interviews and entertainment shows. Full press conferences, behind-the-scenes documentaries, club podcasts or extended match analysis could sit on Instagram, with short Reels acting as trailers that drive fans to the complete video.

INSTAGRAM 5 November 2026 SocialGeek News

Instagram tests Features, horizontal videos up to 90 minutes linked from Reels

Instagram is trialling a format called Features that lets creators publish horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes, aimed at podcasts, interviews and entertainment shows. Reels can link straight to the full episode, and a Clips as Reels option cuts segments from a Feature into Reels. Features can surface in Explore and recommendations, and Instagram wants to bring them to TV screens.

The test is limited to a group of US creators after an earlier trial in Brazil. Monetization remains unclear: unlike YouTube, Instagram has not announced any ad revenue sharing for the format yet, and the move is widely seen as a direct challenge to YouTube's long-form dominance.

INSTAGRAM 2 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta's creator assistant adds trend alerts, storyboard help and AI comment replies

Meta has expanded its AI-powered creator assistant, first launched in June to guide creators on Facebook and Instagram. The tool can now proactively flag trends and opportunities relevant to a creator's niche, turn an idea into a storyboard and shot list, and review a draft before publishing with specific feedback. It also surfaces the most relevant viewer questions in comments and drafts replies in the creator's own voice.

The assistant is now accessible on the web through Meta's Creator Dashboard, making it easier to fit into desktop-based content workflows. Meta says it plans to bring the feature to more apps and tools where creators build content and plan their output, extending its AI guidance further across the production process.
