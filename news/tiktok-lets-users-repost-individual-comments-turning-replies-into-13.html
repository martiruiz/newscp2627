TikTok lets users repost individual comments, turning replies into standalone content

Social News · 22 October 2026 · Source: SocialGeek News

TikTok is rolling out a feature that allows people to repost a single comment without sharing the original video. Funny, useful or interesting comments can now earn reach on their own, and replies can also be discovered outside the thread where they first appeared. The comment section effectively becomes a content format in its own right rather than a secondary space beneath the video.

For sports social teams, this makes community management a direct growth lever. A sharp reply from the club account, a witty reaction from a player, or a standout fan comment after a big result can now travel independently and reach audiences who never saw the original clip. That raises the value of having fast, on-brand responders ready during matches and key moments.

Clubs and leagues could deliberately prompt conversation with questions, predictions or debates in captions, then amplify the best fan answers to reward supporters and fuel engagement. Sponsors could also be woven into recurring comment-driven formats. Equally, teams should keep moderation tight, since a careless or controversial reply could now spread well beyond its thread.

TikTok upgrades Symphony AI ad tools with latest Dreamina Seedance video model

TikTok has expanded its Symphony AI ad creation suite. The Symphony Agent now taps the newest Dreamina Seedance models, letting advertisers upload up to 50 multimodal references (images, video and audio) instead of 9, and generate clips of up to 30 seconds per prompt, double the earlier 15-second cap. TikTok says output is sharper, with better lighting and more consistent characters across frames.

TikTok also released an updated Symphony API so advertisers can run Symphony generation inside their own creative management tools. In addition, starting November 9, advertisers outside North America can buy BytePlus tokens to access Seedance through Symphony. BytePlus is the enterprise tech arm of parent company ByteDance and runs its AI systems.

TIKTOK 2 November 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok launches brand safety center with new counterfeit and copyright protection tools

TikTok has rolled out a new brand safety center website that gathers its guidance for marketers in one place, including videos with internal experts, a new Brand Protection Playbook and an AI Safety Essentials overview. The announcement came at its APAC Responsible Media Forum in Singapore, alongside a responsible AI framework for brands using artificial intelligence.

New tools include a brand perception insights feature inside TikTok's business center, options to report copyright and trademark infringement, and counterfeit protections for sellers. TikTok also shared moderation data, saying it removed over 30 million videos across 10 Asia Pacific markets between April and June 2026, with automated systems handling 97.4% of removals globally.

TIKTOK 2 November 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok formalizes WTA partnership with exclusive livestreams and a new Creator Club

TikTok has signed a formal content deal with the Women's Tennis Association after a year of collaboration that pushed the WTA account past one million followers. WTA content generated a combined 250 million views and engagements over the past year, up 41% year over year. The first activation is an exclusive TikTok livestream of the WTA Finals Draw Ceremony on November 3, ahead of the season-ending event in Indian Wells.

The agreement also launches a WTA Creator Club, giving digital creators access to tournaments worldwide to film behind-the-scenes and off-court stories. The WTA will also gain tournament content hubs through TikTok's GamePlan offering, and both partners say player-led storytelling stays central, with support for athletes building their own presence on the app. TikTok signed a similar deal with the ATP last year.
