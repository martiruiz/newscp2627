Bryson DeChambeau's Backyard Games with Golf Clash draws 83.5M views across five platforms

Social News · 15 October 2026 · Source: SponCon Sports

Bryson DeChambeau partnered with EA's Golf Clash on Backyard Games, a daily series in which 10 golf creators competed on a three-hole course he built at home, with the winner facing him for $100,000. According to Mondo Metrics data cited by SponCon Sports, the campaign produced 83.5 million views, 3.61 million engagements and a 4.32% engagement rate across 64 posts. A teaser carousel playing on rumours he was 'coming home' earned the most sends.

The integration stood out: branded sets and game-style graphics, six-second ad reads during natural pauses, Instagram Stories with download prompts, and an in-game recreation of the course fans could play. The format grew from an unsponsored 2024 challenge that proved audience appeal before sponsors joined. However, views across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube fell each season, though YouTube Shorts average views per post rose 41% since 2024.

For clubs and athletes, the lesson is to build and test a repeatable format organically, then sell it with real performance data rather than pitching concepts that only exist if funded. Give partners a role that improves the fan experience, place sponsor messages inside the action, use Stories for direct calls to action, and keep the star at the centre with fan rewards to sustain reach.

Gen Z app Posting2 grows with chronological, ad-free, algorithm-free photo feed

Posting2 is a small app built as a throwback to early Instagram: square photos, a chronological feed, no algorithm, no ads and no celebrities, with lots of casual everyday posts. According to Fast Company, it grew from 1,600 beta users in April to 36,671 users by October 21, and Sensor Tower recorded a 42% jump in downloads in September versus August.

The app is still tiny, but it reflects a slice of Gen Z that wants to see friends' posts without algorithmic curation. For clubs and athletes, it is a reminder that raw, imperfect and authentic content is regaining value.

SPORTS MARKETING 28 October 2026 Social Media Today

Reddit rolls out algorithmic For You feed to boost community discovery

Reddit is launching a For You feed that uses its engagement data to recommend posts and subreddits users have not yet joined. It sits alongside a Following feed for communities people already belong to. The For You tab gets the prominent first slot, but it is not the default: the app reopens on whichever feed a user last viewed. The rollout is global over the next few weeks.

For sports teams, this shifts Reddit from a closed-community platform toward one where content can reach fans who have never visited a club's subreddit. Discussions around matches, transfers and behind-the-scenes moments could now surface for casual followers of a league or sport, making official and fan-run communities a potential growth channel rather than just a place to serve existing supporters.

SPORTS MARKETING 28 October 2026 Front Office Sports

Women's sports bars push back on WNBA's 12:30 p.m. Finals Game 6 tip-off

The WNBA moved Game 6 of its Finals from 8 p.m. to 12:30 p.m. ET on a Thursday so the Atlanta Dream could play at the 17,000-seat State Farm Arena instead of the 3,500-seat Gateway Center. With Golden State up 3-2, a potential title clincher now starts at 9:30 a.m. in San Francisco. Owners of women's sports bars in Chicago, Brooklyn, Portland and Seattle told Front Office Sports the slot hurts fans and their revenue.

Several owners voiced frustration publicly, starting with an Instagram post from Level Sporting Club's founder. The Sports Bra's founder warned that weaker viewing numbers could be misread as proof the audience is missing. Seattle's Rough & Tumble said it may not open, risking roughly $10,000 to $15,000 per Finals night. Level Sporting Club chose to open anyway, promoting the game on social as a remote-work gathering, a first weekday morning opening for the bar.
