Snapchat user growth stalls in North America and Europe while Spotlight surges

Social News · 5 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

Snap's Q3 2026 results show Snapchat reaching 495 million daily active users, just 2 million more than the previous quarter, with zero growth in North America and Europe. Revenue still rose 14% year over year to $1.72 billion. Teen social media bans are a growing risk: in Australia, Snapchat locked or disabled more than 415,000 accounts belonging to under-16 users.

The bright spot is short-form video. Snap says the number of people posting to Spotlight grew 130% year over year and daily active viewers rose 40%, while messaging and two-way communication also climbed. Subscriptions across Snapchat+, Lens+ and Memories Storage passed 30 million, and Snap is expanding creator partnership options for brands.

For clubs and leagues, Snapchat remains a strong channel for reaching teen fans in Western markets, but audience growth there has plateaued and age restrictions may shrink it. Teams should prioritise Spotlight clips and AR Lenses over chasing reach, track youth-ban developments in their key markets, and consider testing creator collaborations to keep younger supporters engaged.

FACEBOOK 19 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta tests Achados, a Brazil-only shopping app merging Facebook and Instagram listings

Meta has launched Achados, an experimental marketplace app currently available only in Brazil and only on Android. It pulls product listings from Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Groups and items sellers have shared on Instagram into one feed of what people nearby are selling. The app adds price comparisons against similar products across Meta's apps, plus seller profiles, customer reviews and sales histories.

Achados is one of several experimental apps Meta has released this year, though most of its tests see limited use and mainly inform features in its core apps. The launch comes shortly after Meta extended its Creator Fast Track program to Brazilian creators, a sign the company sees growing opportunity in the country.

FACEBOOK 19 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta tests Achados, a Brazil-only shopping app merging Marketplace, Groups and Instagram listings

Meta has released Achados, an experimental shopping app currently available only on Android in Brazil. It pulls items for sale from Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Groups and Instagram sellers into a single local feed. It adds price comparisons against similar products across Meta's apps, plus seller profiles, customer reviews and sales histories. It is one of several new apps Meta has launched this year.

For sports organisations, the signal is Meta's push to treat Instagram and Facebook posts as shoppable inventory. Clubs, fan shops and sports brands in Brazil that already sell merchandise, tickets-adjacent products or matchday gear through Instagram or Facebook could gain extra visibility without building new storefronts. Because listings sit next to price comparisons, consistent pricing and clear product posts become more important.
