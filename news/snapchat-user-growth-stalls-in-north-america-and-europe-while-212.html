Snapchat

Snapchat user growth stalls in North America and Europe while Spotlight surges

Social News · 5 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

Snap's Q3 2026 results show Snapchat reaching 495 million daily active users, just 2 million more than the previous quarter, with zero growth in North America and Europe. Revenue still rose 14% year over year to $1.72 billion. Teen social media bans are a growing risk: in Australia, Snapchat locked or disabled more than 415,000 accounts belonging to under-16 users.

The bright spot is short-form video. Snap says the number of people posting to Spotlight grew 130% year over year and daily active viewers rose 40%, while messaging and two-way communication also climbed. Subscriptions across Snapchat+, Lens+ and Memories Storage passed 30 million, and Snap is expanding creator partnership options for brands.

For clubs and leagues, Snapchat remains a strong channel for reaching teen fans in Western markets, but audience growth there has plateaued and age restrictions may shrink it. Teams should prioritise Spotlight clips and AR Lenses over chasing reach, track youth-ban developments in their key markets, and consider testing creator collaborations to keep younger supporters engaged.

INSTAGRAM 16 November 2026 Social Media Today

Instagram launches dedicated app for Apple's foldable iPhone Duo with side-by-side Reels

Instagram has released a version of its app built specifically for the iPhone Duo, using the device's larger unfolded display. The main feed opens in a wider format, Reels show the video and its comments next to each other while playback continues, control buttons move to a sidebar, and messages open in a two-column layout. When the phone is folded, Stories and Reels keep their usual vertical format.

For sports social teams, the side-by-side Reels view puts the comment section on equal footing with the clip itself. That makes it worth seeding conversation early: pinned replies from the club account, prompts asking fans to predict a result or rate a goal, and quick responses to top comments can all become part of the viewing experience rather than an afterthought.

X 16 November 2026 Social Media Today

X tests work experience section that lets users list career history on profiles

X is experimenting with a work experience element on user profiles, letting people add their professional history, according to images shared by app researcher Radu Oncescu. X has not officially announced the feature. It echoes the platform's earlier job push: job listings for verified organizations launched in August 2023, and X reported more than 750,000 open roles by June 2024, though its hiring profile and job search have since gone dormant.

The test may signal a renewed attempt to compete with LinkedIn and reconnect job seekers with brands. A more skeptical reading is that X wants richer professional data, potentially feeding audience insights into xAI. Either way, profiles on X could soon carry more career context, changing how accounts present themselves and how users judge credibility.

YOUTUBE 16 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Chargers and American Airlines draw 485K YouTube views with road-trip catering episode

The Los Angeles Chargers released a new episode of their American Airlines-backed YouTube series Everything It Takes, this time following how an NFL team gets fed on the way to a road game. The 15-minute film covers meals being loaded onto the plane and how players stay fuelled across the trip, with the airline appearing naturally throughout alongside on-screen branding.

Despite a difficult season on the field, the episode passed 485K YouTube views within four days. The lesson for sports social teams is that a strong behind-the-scenes concept can carry audience interest regardless of results, and that a sponsor earns more credible exposure when it is woven into the story itself rather than added as a logo or a pre-roll mention.
