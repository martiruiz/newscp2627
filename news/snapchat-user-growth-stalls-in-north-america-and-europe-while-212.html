Snapchat

Snapchat user growth stalls in North America and Europe while Spotlight surges

Social News · 5 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

Snap's Q3 2026 results show Snapchat reaching 495 million daily active users, just 2 million more than the previous quarter, with zero growth in North America and Europe. Revenue still rose 14% year over year to $1.72 billion. Teen social media bans are a growing risk: in Australia, Snapchat locked or disabled more than 415,000 accounts belonging to under-16 users.

The bright spot is short-form video. Snap says the number of people posting to Spotlight grew 130% year over year and daily active viewers rose 40%, while messaging and two-way communication also climbed. Subscriptions across Snapchat+, Lens+ and Memories Storage passed 30 million, and Snap is expanding creator partnership options for brands.

For clubs and leagues, Snapchat remains a strong channel for reaching teen fans in Western markets, but audience growth there has plateaued and age restrictions may shrink it. Teams should prioritise Spotlight clips and AR Lenses over chasing reach, track youth-ban developments in their key markets, and consider testing creator collaborations to keep younger supporters engaged.

Aaron Rodgers' McAfee Show interview shows the power of athlete-friendly platforms

Aaron Rodgers used The Pat McAfee Show to respond to being benched by the Pittsburgh Steelers, delivering a pointed critique of head coach Mike McCarthy. With ESPN and ESPN2 busy with college basketball, the TV broadcast moved to ESPNews, but the show's YouTube stream had passed 622,000 viewers by press time. The Steelers released Rodgers shortly after the interview.

Front Office Sports frames McAfee as the big winner. His long relationship with Rodgers, and a relaxed format that lets stars talk without pushback, keeps landing interviews that dominate the sports conversation. The piece links that pull to ESPN's reported extension talks with McAfee. It also notes LeBron James chose the same show to answer criticism of his son Bronny.

SPORTS MARKETING 25 November 2026 SponCon Sports

A 60-minute research method for building tailored sponsored content pitches

Sponcon Sports newsletter author Alex Kopilow has outlined a three-step process, collect, compare and customize, for creating brand-specific digital partnership ideas in about an hour. It draws on eight free resources, including secondary social accounts that follow every pro team and league, sports broadcasts as a source of segment names, X search filtered to people you follow, and Meta's Branded Content search and Ad Library.

He also suggests YouTube's Gemini-powered Ask feature to locate sponsor integrations within videos, checking a partner's website, bios and search ads for taglines and priority products, and an AI prompt that finds category terms with double meanings in a given sport, such as linking tire brands to a baseball pitching rotation feature.

AI 24 November 2026 Social Media Today

Emplifi survey: AI boosts social team output without shrinking headcount

Emplifi polled 405 marketing managers, directors and executives about trends shaping their plans for 2027. Ninety percent said AI has raised their team's output, yet 54% reported their social media team actually grew in size rather than shrinking. Sixty-nine percent said AI tools save them at least three hours each week on reporting, and 55% expect short-form video to keep gaining relevance and influence.

For club, league and athlete social teams, the data supports treating AI as a capacity multiplier rather than a replacement. Automating routine work such as weekly performance reports, sponsor recaps and post-match analytics can free hours for creative tasks like matchday storytelling, player-led content and fan interaction. That reclaimed time is also a useful argument when making the case for team investment to leadership.
