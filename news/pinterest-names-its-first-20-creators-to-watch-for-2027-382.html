Pinterest names its first 20 Creators to Watch for 2027

Social News · 17 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

Pinterest has released its inaugural list of 20 global creators to watch in 2027, published as part of its wider trend forecast for the coming year. The selection spans categories such as fashion, beauty and home, and is meant to show users and marketers which trend setters are shaping activity on the platform. Pinterest says 83% of its users feel in-app trends are informed by expert opinions.

The list arrives a month after Pinterest began letting brands sponsor creator Pins, giving marketers a paid route to amplify creator content. Together, the two moves signal that Pinterest wants brands to treat its creators as campaign partners, not just sources of inspiration.

For sports teams and sports brands, this is a useful shortlist for lifestyle-led partnerships. A club launching a new kit, fan merchandise or a matchday hospitality offer could brief a fashion or home creator to style products in an authentic way, then use sponsored creator Pins to extend reach. Athletes with lifestyle brands can also watch these creators to spot emerging aesthetics before they go mainstream.

FACEBOOK 19 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta tests Achados, a Brazil-only shopping app merging Facebook and Instagram listings

Meta has launched Achados, an experimental marketplace app currently available only in Brazil and only on Android. It pulls product listings from Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Groups and items sellers have shared on Instagram into one feed of what people nearby are selling. The app adds price comparisons against similar products across Meta's apps, plus seller profiles, customer reviews and sales histories.

Achados is one of several experimental apps Meta has released this year, though most of its tests see limited use and mainly inform features in its core apps. The launch comes shortly after Meta extended its Creator Fast Track program to Brazilian creators, a sign the company sees growing opportunity in the country.
