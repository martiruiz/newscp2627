Pinterest names its first 20 Creators to Watch for 2027

Social News · 17 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

Pinterest has released its inaugural list of 20 global creators to watch in 2027, published as part of its wider trend forecast for the coming year. The selection spans categories such as fashion, beauty and home, and is meant to show users and marketers which trend setters are shaping activity on the platform. Pinterest says 83% of its users feel in-app trends are informed by expert opinions.

The list arrives a month after Pinterest began letting brands sponsor creator Pins, giving marketers a paid route to amplify creator content. Together, the two moves signal that Pinterest wants brands to treat its creators as campaign partners, not just sources of inspiration.

For sports teams and sports brands, this is a useful shortlist for lifestyle-led partnerships. A club launching a new kit, fan merchandise or a matchday hospitality offer could brief a fashion or home creator to style products in an authentic way, then use sponsored creator Pins to extend reach. Athletes with lifestyle brands can also watch these creators to spot emerging aesthetics before they go mainstream.

Kirk Herbstreit distances himself from viral SEC Shorts sketch mocking ESPN and CFP

Comedy group SEC Shorts posted a roughly two-and-a-half-minute sketch on X lampooning the College Football Playoff committee and ESPN's rankings show, including a parody of analyst Heather Dinich. The video passed 2 million views and drew thousands of replies in under nine hours. ESPN's Kirk Herbstreit appears at the end delivering an ad read for Renasant Bank, the presenting sponsor of every SEC Shorts video.

Herbstreit told Front Office Sports he works with Renasant, which uses him on SEC Shorts' weekly clips, but has no partnership with the group and no role in its content. ESPN declined to comment, while Dinich appeared to respond with a light jab promoting her upcoming book. The episode shows how a sponsor's ad placement can tie talent to content they never approved.

SPORTS MARKETING 23 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Dr Pepper reaches college football fans through House of Highlights' CLASH debate series

Dr Pepper has found a way into college football conversations without partnering with a team or league. The brand worked with CLASH, the debate and trivia channel from House of Highlights, on three episodes featuring coach Ed Orgeron, creator MMG and former Heisman winner Troy Smith. Alongside product placement and branded messaging, the activation added merchandise QR codes and paid ads running against the videos, pushing views past 2 million.

The lesson for sports marketers is that fan attention increasingly sits with media brands and creators, not only official rights holders. Brands can build relevance in a sport by sponsoring the shows and debate formats fans already watch, then layering commerce hooks such as QR codes and paid amplification to extend reach and measure response.

X 22 November 2026 Social Media Today

X adds custom video thumbnails, new caption styles and one-tap vertical formatting

X has rolled out a new set of video creation tools: more caption styles and colours, the ability to manually pick a thumbnail frame from an uploaded clip, extra text overlays, and layout options that convert horizontal footage to vertical in one tap using Fill, Fade or Stack. X says posts with video get five times the reach and engagement, and that 36% of all impressions now go to video.

For sports social teams, this makes X more viable for repurposing broadcast-style horizontal highlights into vertical clips without a separate edit. Choosing the thumbnail means a club can lead with the celebration, the save or the player's reaction rather than a random frame, which matters for in-stream stopping power during busy matchdays.
