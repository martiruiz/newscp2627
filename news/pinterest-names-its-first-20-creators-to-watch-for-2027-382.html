Pinterest names its first 20 Creators to Watch for 2027

Social News · 17 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

Pinterest has released its inaugural list of 20 global creators to watch in 2027, published as part of its wider trend forecast for the coming year. The selection spans categories such as fashion, beauty and home, and is meant to show users and marketers which trend setters are shaping activity on the platform. Pinterest says 83% of its users feel in-app trends are informed by expert opinions.

The list arrives a month after Pinterest began letting brands sponsor creator Pins, giving marketers a paid route to amplify creator content. Together, the two moves signal that Pinterest wants brands to treat its creators as campaign partners, not just sources of inspiration.

For sports teams and sports brands, this is a useful shortlist for lifestyle-led partnerships. A club launching a new kit, fan merchandise or a matchday hospitality offer could brief a fashion or home creator to style products in an authentic way, then use sponsored creator Pins to extend reach. Athletes with lifestyle brands can also watch these creators to spot emerging aesthetics before they go mainstream.

Aaron Rodgers' McAfee Show interview shows the power of athlete-friendly platforms

Aaron Rodgers used The Pat McAfee Show to respond to being benched by the Pittsburgh Steelers, delivering a pointed critique of head coach Mike McCarthy. SPORTS MARKETING 25 November 2026 SponCon Sports

A 60-minute research method for building tailored sponsored content pitches

Sponcon Sports newsletter author Alex Kopilow has outlined a three-step process, collect, compare and customize, for creating brand-specific digital partnership ideas in about an hour. It draws on eight free resources, including secondary social accounts that follow every pro team and league, sports broadcasts as a source of segment names, X search filtered to people you follow, and Meta's Branded Content search and Ad Library.

He also suggests YouTube's Gemini-powered Ask feature to locate sponsor integrations within videos, checking a partner's website, bios and search ads for taglines and priority products, and an AI prompt that finds category terms with double meanings in a given sport, such as linking tire brands to a baseball pitching rotation feature.

AI 24 November 2026 Social Media Today

Emplifi survey: AI boosts social team output without shrinking headcount

Emplifi polled 405 marketing managers, directors and executives about trends shaping their plans for 2027. Ninety percent said AI has raised their team's output, yet 54% reported their social media team actually grew in size rather than shrinking. Sixty-nine percent said AI tools save them at least three hours each week on reporting, and 55% expect short-form video to keep gaining relevance and influence.

For club, league and athlete social teams, the data supports treating AI as a capacity multiplier rather than a replacement. Automating routine work such as weekly performance reports, sponsor recaps and post-match analytics can free hours for creative tasks like matchday storytelling, player-led content and fan interaction. That reclaimed time is also a useful argument when making the case for team investment to leadership.
