TikTok formalizes WTA partnership with exclusive livestreams and a new Creator Club

Social News · 2 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

TikTok has signed a formal content deal with the Women's Tennis Association after a year of collaboration that pushed the WTA account past one million followers. WTA content generated a combined 250 million views and engagements over the past year, up 41% year over year. The first activation is an exclusive TikTok livestream of the WTA Finals Draw Ceremony on November 3, ahead of the season-ending event in Indian Wells.

The agreement also launches a WTA Creator Club, giving digital creators access to tournaments worldwide to film behind-the-scenes and off-court stories. The WTA will also gain tournament content hubs through TikTok's GamePlan offering, and both partners say player-led storytelling stays central, with support for athletes building their own presence on the app. TikTok signed a similar deal with the ATP last year.

For sports social teams, this is a template worth studying: turn low-cost moments like draws or ceremonies into live events, invite creators inside the venue to reach audiences beyond core fans, and help athletes show personality across fashion and lifestyle. Leagues and federations negotiating with platforms can use these engagement figures to argue for formal partnerships, exclusive formats and dedicated content hubs.

TikTok launches brand safety center with new counterfeit and copyright protection tools

TikTok has rolled out a new brand safety center website that gathers its guidance for marketers in one place, including videos with internal experts, a new Brand Protection Playbook and an AI Safety Essentials overview. The announcement came at its APAC Responsible Media Forum in Singapore, alongside a responsible AI framework for brands using artificial intelligence.

New tools include a brand perception insights feature inside TikTok's business center, options to report copyright and trademark infringement, and counterfeit protections for sellers. TikTok also shared moderation data, saying it removed over 30 million videos across 10 Asia Pacific markets between April and June 2026, with automated systems handling 97.4% of removals globally.

TIKTOK 28 October 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok launches Must Eat, an in-app restaurant guide built on community recommendations

TikTok has rolled out Must Eat, a restaurant guide inside the app covering several U.S. regions. Users can browse by city and filter by cuisine, price, score and location. Each restaurant page shows neighborhood details, price ranges and related TikTok videos. Some venues can be booked directly through OpenTable, and spots can be saved to existing TikTok collections. The feature extends the local feed TikTok introduced in February.

TikTok says 27 million Americans visited an independent restaurant, cafe or shop for the first time last year after finding it on the platform. The company also points to Douyin, its Chinese counterpart, which moved from a restaurant guide into in-app food ordering, suggesting TikTok may push further into local commerce.

TIKTOK 25 October 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok US removes 14.6 million fake accounts and 781 million fake followers in Q2

TikTok USDS, the US-based group running the app's American operations since January, released its second enforcement report, covering April to June. It removed 14.6 million fake accounts, blocked 64.8 million spam accounts at sign-up, stripped 781.4 million fake followers and stopped 403.2 million fake follow requests, a clear jump on the first quarter. Video removals and livestream warnings or demonetizations also rose.

Most video takedowns fell under regulated goods and services, a broad category covering gambling, alcohol, counterfeit products and services selling fake likes, views and followers. TikTok also said it disrupted 30 covert networks trying to manipulate engagement, removing more than 76,000 accounts and 4.8 million posts, as it ramps up integrity efforts ahead of the US midterms.
