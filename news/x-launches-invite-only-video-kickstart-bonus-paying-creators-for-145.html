X launches invite-only Video Kickstart bonus paying creators for regular vertical video

Social News · 1 November 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

X has introduced Video Kickstart, an invite-only pilot that pays creators a bonus for posting original short-form vertical videos. To qualify in a roughly month-long period, participants must publish 15 eligible clips on 15 separate days, each under three minutes. Content first shared on other platforms is allowed as long as the creator owns it, and enrollees are fast-tracked into X's ad revenue sharing program.

The move continues X's push into video, following a revamped livestream studio, new overlay options and an updated video composer. For sports social teams, it signals that X will likely reward native vertical video with more visibility, making it worth repurposing owned clips from TikTok, Reels or Shorts rather than only posting links and text updates.

Athletes and club creators who receive an invitation could build a simple posting rhythm of training clips, behind-the-scenes moments and matchday reactions to hit the 15-day threshold. Clubs and leagues should check eligibility in Creator Studio, track enrollment deadlines, and review how ad revenue sharing fits with existing sponsor and rights agreements before monetizing footage.

Kirk Herbstreit distances himself from viral SEC Shorts sketch mocking ESPN and CFP

Comedy group SEC Shorts posted a roughly two-and-a-half-minute sketch on X lampooning the College Football Playoff committee and ESPN's rankings show, including a parody of analyst Heather Dinich. The video passed 2 million views and drew thousands of replies in under nine hours. ESPN's Kirk Herbstreit appears at the end delivering an ad read for Renasant Bank, the presenting sponsor of every SEC Shorts video.

Herbstreit told Front Office Sports he works with Renasant, which uses him on SEC Shorts' weekly clips, but has no partnership with the group and no role in its content. ESPN declined to comment, while Dinich appeared to respond with a light jab promoting her upcoming book. The episode shows how a sponsor's ad placement can tie talent to content they never approved.

X 22 November 2026 Social Media Today

X adds custom video thumbnails, new caption styles and one-tap vertical formatting

X has rolled out a new set of video creation tools: more caption styles and colours, the ability to manually pick a thumbnail frame from an uploaded clip, extra text overlays, and layout options that convert horizontal footage to vertical in one tap using Fill, Fade or Stack. X says posts with video get five times the reach and engagement, and that 36% of all impressions now go to video.

For sports social teams, this makes X more viable for repurposing broadcast-style horizontal highlights into vertical clips without a separate edit. Choosing the thumbnail means a club can lead with the celebration, the save or the player's reaction rather than a random frame, which matters for in-stream stopping power during busy matchdays.

X 16 November 2026 Social Media Today

X tests work experience section that lets users list career history on profiles

X is experimenting with a work experience element on user profiles, letting people add their professional history, according to images shared by app researcher Radu Oncescu. X has not officially announced the feature. It echoes the platform's earlier job push: job listings for verified organizations launched in August 2023, and X reported more than 750,000 open roles by June 2024, though its hiring profile and job search have since gone dormant.

The test may signal a renewed attempt to compete with LinkedIn and reconnect job seekers with brands. A more skeptical reading is that X wants richer professional data, potentially feeding audience insights into xAI. Either way, profiles on X could soon carry more career context, changing how accounts present themselves and how users judge credibility.
