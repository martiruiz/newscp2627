Mosseri explains Instagram ranking signals: engagement rate, timing, originality and testing

Social News · 1 October 2026 · Source: SocialGeek News

Instagram head Adam Mosseri has released new videos breaking down how the platform decides what to show users. His key points: interaction rates matter more than raw views or likes, posting time still counts because the feed stays roughly chronological, original content is prioritised over reposts, and small accounts can be shown to non-followers to test their reach. He also noted that AI models increasingly drive ranking.

For club and league social teams, the message is to judge posts by engagement rate rather than view counts, and to schedule content around when fans are actually online, such as kick-off, half-time and full-time windows. Reposting broadcaster clips or fan videos is likely to be outranked by the original creator, so in-house footage from training, the tunnel and the dressing room should carry more weight.

Mosseri also stressed constant experimentation, since what works one week may not work the next. Trial Reels let teams test formats with non-followers without risking their main feed, which suits matchday experiments, player challenges or new series. Instagram has also published a 2026 Reels creation guide covering editing, music, captions, Templates, Remix and Collab, offering a useful reference for refreshing workflows.

Meta's Edits app adds Halloween effects, beat-synced animations and bulk caption editing

Meta has rolled out a new batch of updates to Edits, its video editing app tied to Instagram. The release includes Halloween-themed fonts, sound effects and animations, 13 new text animations, and beat-synced effects such as flashing lights and zooms that automatically match a clip's music. Caption tools gain extra styles and bulk editing, while new automated color correction and per-clip volume boosts up to 150% round out the update.

Edits' carousel editor, launched on iOS earlier this month, is now also available on Android, and a new filter was created with Nigerian musician Arya Starr. Meta says a desktop version is coming soon, which would move the app closer to being a full production studio rather than a quick mobile editor.

INSTAGRAM 27 October 2026 Social Media Today

Australia weighs algorithm opt-out rule as Instagram's Mosseri warns of shrinking reach

Australia's government has started early consultation on rules that would require social platforms to offer a switch to turn off algorithmic recommendations, with a non-algorithmic, following-only feed as the default. Communications Minister Annika Wells argues engagement-driven feeds reward attention over well-being, and says platforms must respect a user's choice rather than burying it. Nothing has been formally tabled yet, and the proposal is still open for community input.

Instagram chief Adam Mosseri has pushed back, saying on Threads that a following-only feed would mean a smaller Instagram and less reach for everyone, including creators. Separately, commentator Michael Shellenberger has framed the plan as a speech-control measure, though key provisions he criticised have been removed or are being revised during consultation.

INSTAGRAM 26 October 2026 Social Media Today

Instagram tests 90-minute Features videos, Clips as Reels and Series for creators

Instagram has started testing a set of long-form video tools. Features lets selected creators publish videos of up to 90 minutes, suited to podcasts, vlogs and interviews, and is expanding from Brazil to select US creators from October 26. Clips as Reels lets creators link up to eight Reels back to a longer video, while Series groups episodic Reels under a single theme. Phone-as-remote control is also coming to Instagram's connected TV app.

For sports teams, this opens the door to hosting full press conferences, behind-the-scenes documentaries, club podcasts or extended player interviews directly on Instagram, where fan communities already live. Clips as Reels is a natural fit for cutting short highlights that funnel fans into a full matchday film, and Series can package recurring formats like weekly training diaries or a season-long docuseries.
