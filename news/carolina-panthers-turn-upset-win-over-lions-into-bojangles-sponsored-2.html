Carolina Panthers Turn Upset Win Over Lions Into Bojangles Sponsored Trash Talk

Social News · 12 October 2026 · Source: SponCon Sports

After beating the Detroit Lions in an upset, the Carolina Panthers called out the analysts who had picked against them, and they gave the moment a sponsor. Under a concept called Keep The Receipts, Bojangles backed the content and offered fans a buy-one-get-one deal on large Legendary Iced Teas using the code "GetTheTea," connecting the team's celebration directly to a purchase.

The idea shows that reactive, emotional moments can be packaged as sponsor inventory instead of staying purely organic posts. Clubs can prepare sponsored templates ahead of time for upsets, milestones or rivalry wins, pairing them with a partner whose product or slang fits the message. Adding a promo code turns spikes in fan attention into measurable sales, giving partners clear results to justify renewals.

The same newsletter highlights similar plays: Anthony Edwards brought back his Believe That Awards with PrizePicks as presenting sponsor, a dedicated Instagram account and fan predictions, while the New York Jets extended a tifo activation with Atlantic Health into text-to-win merchandise that also grows their SMS audience. The common lesson is to build original formats that partners can own and that push fans to act.

PGA Tour Lands Short-Term Sponsor for Austin Event After Good Good Golf Exit

The PGA Tour has found a partial replacement for Good Good Golf, the creator group that withdrew from sponsoring and hosting a new Austin tournament after backlash over a controversial Callaway driver ad. Compliance Solutions, a Florida tax services firm already backing a Korn Ferry Tour event, will become presenting sponsor of the renamed Austin Championship for this year only, paying less than the $6 million Good Good had committed.

The reduced fee reflects how little time remains to activate: practice rounds start in about 11 days, leaving the sponsor almost no runway for promotion. Meanwhile, Good Good has also lost its Callaway deal, its Golf Channel show and retail placement for its merchandise, showing how quickly a creator brand's commercial ecosystem can unravel after a misstep.

NBA opening week sponsored content report: what top teams did with partners

A Sponcon Sports analysis with Mondo Metrics reviewed 11,919 posts from all 30 NBA teams across five platforms during opening week. About 11.7% carried a brand integration, producing 5.6M engagements and at least 42M views for 193 brands. The 76ers led sponsored engagement, Golden State dominated views thanks to a Steph Curry warmup trick shot backed by IREN, and Michelob ULTRA was the most featured sponsor, shifting toward a Courtside Moments series.

Standout ideas include jersey patch partners running surveys and uniform votes to gather first-party data, the Sixers' Bloom Energy 'Power Run' giveaway pushed via Instagram Stories, and series names that link naturally to the sponsor, such as Hornets x Lowe's 'Drilling Threes'. Swapping 'presented by' for phrases like 'Delivered By FedEx' and embedding promo codes in win graphics also added brand meaning without standalone ads.

Gen Z app Posting2 grows with chronological, ad-free, algorithm-free photo feed

Posting2 is a small app built as a throwback to early Instagram: square photos, a chronological feed, no algorithm, no ads and no celebrities, with lots of casual everyday posts. According to Fast Company, it grew from 1,600 beta users in April to 36,671 users by October 21, and Sensor Tower recorded a 42% jump in downloads in September versus August.

The app is still tiny, but it reflects a slice of Gen Z that wants to see friends' posts without algorithmic curation. For clubs and athletes, it is a reminder that raw, imperfect and authentic content is regaining value.
