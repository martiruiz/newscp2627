X shares holiday ad guidance: short copy, natural tone and one clear call to action

Social News · 25 October 2026 · Source: Social Media Today

X hosted a webinar for marketers planning holiday campaigns, sharing audience insights and creative best practices. The platform says its top-performing ads run just 50 to 100 characters, roughly one sentence. It urges brands to sound like real people rather than overselling, to give users a reason to act now through limited-time or exclusive offers, and to finish with a single, clear call to action.

X also advised removing anything that distracts from the ad's goal: send users to one destination, avoid tagging other accounts, and skip hashtags and multiple emojis, which are not allowed in ads. On AI-driven shopping, it recommended describing plainly what a product does, who it is for and why it is better, so recommendation models can summarise it easily.

For clubs and leagues selling holiday merchandise, gift memberships or ticket packages, this is a practical checklist. Write one punchy line, point fans only to the store or app, and use deadlines like limited kit drops or festive ticket bundles to create urgency. Clear, plain product descriptions on e-commerce pages may also help club merchandise surface in AI-powered shopping recommendations.

Manchester United and Snapdragon turn Champions League Starting XI graphics into illustrated portraits

Manchester United has commissioned Stanley Chow, a local illustrator and lifelong supporter, to draw custom portraits of the club's Starting XI for its Champions League fixtures this season. Each player appears in the Snapdragon-branded kit, and the artwork is photographed on Snapdragon-powered phones against scenic backdrops in host cities such as Como and Madrid.

On Instagram, United is pairing the carousel with the platform's new multiple captions feature, labelling each player as fans swipe through the portraits. The approach gives a routine matchday asset a premium, collectible feel while weaving the sponsor's product into the story rather than simply stamping a logo on a template.

Meta's Edits app launches iPhone Duo version and tests desktop editor

Meta has released a dedicated version of Edits, its short-form video editing app, built for Apple's folding iPhone Duo. The interface adapts to the device's larger screen, orientation and crease, letting users edit and preview their timeline side by side. Separately, a desktop version first announced in June is now in live testing with selected creators, according to a screenshot shared by creator Taylor Golub.

The desktop layout shows more editing elements on one screen, making it easier to move between the app's creation tools. TIKTOK 25 October 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok US removes over 14 million fake accounts in second-quarter enforcement report

TikTok has released account enforcement data for its US operation covering the second quarter. The platform says it removed more than 14 million fake accounts during the period. It also says it blocked close to 65 million spam accounts before they could be created, stopping them at the sign-up stage.

For sports social teams, large fake-account purges can cause sudden dips in follower counts and engagement figures. Clubs, leagues and athletes should expect occasional drops and avoid treating them as a loss of real fans. Benchmarking against engaged metrics such as watch time, shares and comments gives a more reliable picture than raw follower totals.
