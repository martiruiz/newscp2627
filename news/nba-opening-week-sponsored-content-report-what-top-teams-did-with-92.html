NBA opening week sponsored content report: what top teams did with partners

Social News · 29 October 2026 · Source: SponCon Sports

A Sponcon Sports analysis with Mondo Metrics reviewed 11,919 posts from all 30 NBA teams across five platforms during opening week. About 11.7% carried a brand integration, producing 5.6M engagements and at least 42M views for 193 brands. The 76ers led sponsored engagement, Golden State dominated views thanks to a Steph Curry warmup trick shot backed by IREN, and Michelob ULTRA was the most featured sponsor, shifting toward a Courtside Moments series.

Standout ideas include jersey patch partners running surveys and uniform votes to gather first-party data, the Sixers' Bloom Energy 'Power Run' giveaway pushed via Instagram Stories, and series names that link naturally to the sponsor, such as Hornets x Lowe's 'Drilling Threes'. Swapping 'presented by' for phrases like 'Delivered By FedEx' and embedding promo codes in win graphics also added brand meaning without standalone ads.

The report flags a common flaw: sponsor logos too small or low-contrast to read on mobile. A simple fix for content teams is previewing creative on private accounts at real size before partner approval. Clubs in any league can borrow these tactics to make sponsored posts feel native, keep brands recognizable and build measurable value for renewals.

Tobin Heath and Christen Press Land Multi-Year Ally Deal, Discuss Athlete-Led Media

Former USWNT stars Tobin Heath and Christen Press sat down with Front Office Sports for a video interview. They covered their new multi-year, multimillion-dollar partnership with Ally, how far the NWSL has come since its first championship, and their work building athlete-led media and businesses through their company Re-Inc. SPORTS MARKETING 19 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Why sports organizations should align content and partnerships teams to grow sponsorship revenue

In his Sponcon Sports newsletter, consultant Alex Kopilow argues that the most neglected relationship in sports organizations is the one between content and partnerships teams. Drawing on his consulting work and time at the Chicago White Sox, he says sellers often pitch formats content teams cannot deliver, while partnerships staff miss sellable ideas because they never hear what content is planning.

He proposes six changes for 2027: cross-attending each other's weekly meetings, bringing content staff into pitches and discovery sessions, building a shared digital inventory, sharing pitch updates and deal values, educating each other on goals and metrics, and giving the wider marketing department a shared digital sponsorship revenue target with a team bonus, provided audience and content standards are maintained.

SPORTS MARKETING 19 November 2026 SocialGeek News

Twitch brings back Hosting and tests shoppable ads at TwitchCon

At TwitchCon, Twitch announced that Hosting is returning, with tests starting at the end of the year: an offline channel can show another live stream with shared chat, and 'Host Trains' rotate audiences across chosen channels. Streamers will also be able to invite up to seven viewers into Luna games using their phones as controllers, and a new apprentice moderator role can time out users but not ban them.

On the business side, shoppable ads are in desktop beta for US and Canadian advertisers, letting viewers add products to a cart mid-stream. A skippable ad format enters open beta in December, with international shopping planned for early 2027. Twitch says creators landing sponsorships have quadrupled in a year, while total payouts have doubled.
