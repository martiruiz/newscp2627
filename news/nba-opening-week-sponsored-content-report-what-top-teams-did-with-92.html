NBA opening week sponsored content report: what top teams did with partners

Social News · 29 October 2026 · Source: SponCon Sports

A Sponcon Sports analysis with Mondo Metrics reviewed 11,919 posts from all 30 NBA teams across five platforms during opening week. About 11.7% carried a brand integration, producing 5.6M engagements and at least 42M views for 193 brands. The 76ers led sponsored engagement, Golden State dominated views thanks to a Steph Curry warmup trick shot backed by IREN, and Michelob ULTRA was the most featured sponsor, shifting toward a Courtside Moments series.

Standout ideas include jersey patch partners running surveys and uniform votes to gather first-party data, the Sixers' Bloom Energy 'Power Run' giveaway pushed via Instagram Stories, and series names that link naturally to the sponsor, such as Hornets x Lowe's 'Drilling Threes'. Swapping 'presented by' for phrases like 'Delivered By FedEx' and embedding promo codes in win graphics also added brand meaning without standalone ads.

The report flags a common flaw: sponsor logos too small or low-contrast to read on mobile. A simple fix for content teams is previewing creative on private accounts at real size before partner approval. Clubs in any league can borrow these tactics to make sponsored posts feel native, keep brands recognizable and build measurable value for renewals.

SPORTS MARKETING 5 November 2026 SponCon Sports

MLB postseason lifts social engagement per post by 51%, reshaping sponsorship pricing

An analysis by SponCon Sports and Mondo Metrics of 57,239 posts from all 12 MLB postseason clubs found engagements per post rose 51% and views per post 26% versus the regular season. Even after adjusting for teams shifting more content to Instagram and TikTok, engagement still climbed 32%. Facebook showed the largest jump, with engagements up 79%, while World Series clubs averaged a 168% lift compared with 13% for Wild Card exits.

The takeaway for partnership and social teams is that playoff inventory deserves its own pricing, but not a blanket markup. The author recommends each club calculate its own postseason bump by platform and, ideally, by content series, then refresh those benchmarks every year it qualifies, since algorithms and fan behavior shift. Teams without recent playoff data can use the MLB findings as a starting reference.

SPORTS MARKETING 4 November 2026 Front Office Sports

High school athletic directors now run content operations as NIL and short-form video reshape prep sports

Front Office Sports spoke with around a dozen veteran high school and state athletic administrators across the U.S., who say the job has become far more demanding over the past five years. They link the shift to the arrival of NIL alongside the boom in short-form video. ADs like Ponte Vedra's Mike Harrison now sell videoboard ads, produce hype videos for big games and manage promotion of teams and individual athletes on school social channels.

Families increasingly expect instant, individualized exposure. Sidelines are filling with private content creators hired by athletes, and one Ohio AD said programs without highlight reels posted within an hour of a game are seen as falling behind. To keep up, many schools are recruiting student volunteers interested in tech and journalism to capture and share game content. Actual NIL deals remain rare, but ADs expect compliance work to arrive soon.

SPORTS MARKETING 3 November 2026 Social Media Today

Reddit begins testing query-targeted ads inside its in-app search results

Reddit has started testing ads in the search results of its app. The ads are targeted by search query, carry a small ad label at the top of the results panel, and are placed mainly on contextual relevance. The test is open first to a select group of eligible advertisers. Reddit says search pages show strong purchase and search intent, and that half of its users credit the platform with helping them discover something new.

The move comes as Reddit pushes discovery. A July study found that 72% of its users come looking for information and 64% arrive with a specific goal. However, CEO Steve Huffman said in the Q3 update that search referrals fell by more than a million per week on average in October as AI-driven traffic declined, which could affect the value of its discovery features.
