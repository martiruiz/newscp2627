How Akron Football Turned a Viral Ticket Promotion Into Arby's Sponsorship Inventory

Social News · 1 October 2026 · Source: SponCon Sports

Akron football invited one season-ticket holder to help script the opening offensive series of its home opener. The August announcement drew 23 million views on X, sold out suites for the first time in program history, and attracted entries from personalities like Big Cat and Jon Gruden. After the winner was revealed, Arby's Steak Nuggets came on as presenting sponsor, timed to the product's nationwide return as a limited-time offer.

According to Mondo Metrics, campaign posts averaged 2.9x Akron's usual engagement over more than a month, while earned media from outlets like House of Highlights and SportsCenter delivered about 13.5 million views. Game day became an Arby's takeover, with branding on the play-call binder, foam fingers, press conference food and even regular highlights. One critique: a behind-the-scenes Reel that opened at an Arby's underperformed, showing sponsor requests should not override what fans came to see.

The lesson for sports social teams is to treat breakout content as proof for partnerships. When a series, promotion or unsponsored campaign takes off, share the data with sales quickly, since proven performance is easier to sell than a concept. Package tentpole moments as full takeovers across pregame, highlights and postgame, keep the story going in chapters, and consider livestreaming draws with creators to boost reach and credibility.

USC's Jazzy Davidson discusses Venmo NIL campaign and building her personal brand

USC women's basketball standout Jazzy Davidson sat down with Front Office Sports for a video interview covering her partnership with Venmo on its Money Moves campaign and her own habits around managing NIL income. She also talked about her approach to personal branding, being a role model for young fans, playing alongside JuJu Watkins, her pro options across the WNBA, Unrivaled and Project B, and uneven media coverage of women's sports.

For sports social teams, the interview shows how brands are pairing college athletes with practical, values-driven messaging like financial literacy rather than simple product plugs. Athletes who position themselves as role models for kids give sponsors a credible, family-friendly voice, and that kind of story travels well in short video, Q&A and carousel formats.

SPORTS MARKETING 27 October 2026 Front Office Sports

Chinese sponsors drop Mbappé after resurfaced Mao 'dictator' meme comment

Kylian Mbappé has apologized after an old clip resurfaced online in which, asked on French show The Bridge about his favorite meme of himself, he described a face-swap with Mao Zedong as showing a Chinese dictator. Beverage brand Dongpeng, dairy company Mengniu and cheese maker Milkground ended their sponsorship deals, while Chinese state TV pulled LaLiga broadcasts and Mbappé ads. LaLiga president Javier Tebas posted an apology to China on X.

For sports social teams, the episode shows how a light-hearted podcast or show moment can resurface and trigger commercial fallout across markets. It echoes the NBA's 2019 Hong Kong tweet crisis and Brighton's apology over a social post last year. Clubs and athletes with Asian partners face real sponsorship and broadcast risk from content that feels harmless in a home market.

SPORTS MARKETING 26 October 2026 Social Media Today

Pew survey: 58% of US adults now rely on social media for news

A Pew Research report released on Oct. 23, based on a June survey of more than 3,500 US adults, found that 58% consider social media an important way to follow current events. Another 13% name social apps as their single most important source of information. In Pew's 2022 edition of the same study, those figures were 43% and 5%.

For sports social teams, this confirms that club and league accounts increasingly act as primary newsrooms rather than secondary channels. Many fans will first hear about signings, injuries, lineups or ticket news in their feeds, not on a website or through traditional media. Official accounts should prioritize speed, accuracy and clear, native-format announcements so they become the trusted first source.
