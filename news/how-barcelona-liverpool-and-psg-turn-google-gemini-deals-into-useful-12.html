How Barcelona, Liverpool and PSG Turn Google Gemini Deals Into Useful Fan Content

Social News · 22 October 2026 · Source: SponCon Sports

Key takeaways

  • FC Barcelona runs a pre-match Instagram Story series sponsored by Google Gemini that shows a practical use of the product, not just AI output.
  • Text-heavy slides limit results: PSG's similar feed carousels averaged 30.8K engagements across three posts, modest for a club with more than 60M followers.
  • Stronger Gemini activations include Liverpool's new-signing introduction and fan quizzes, and Barcelona's October fixture preview, which passed 482K likes.
  • AI partnership content works best when it is built around what fans want, such as previews, new-signing explainers, quizzes or club history.
  • Folding a sponsor into an established recurring series keeps the activation natural and can create predictable sponsorship inventory.

FC Barcelona is running a pre-match Instagram Story series sponsored by Google Gemini. A four-frame sequence opens with a mock Gemini prompt asking for facts about the next opponent, followed by three frames of answers paired with player imagery. The series is part of the Gemini and Pixel partnerships Google announced in August with Barcelona, PSG, Arsenal, Liverpool and Bayern Munich. A SponCon Sports analysis praises it for showing a practical way to use the product rather than simply displaying AI output.

The analysis also notes limits. The slides are text-heavy, and PSG's similar feed carousels averaged 30.8K engagements across three posts reviewed, modest for a club with more than 60M followers. Other activations have done more. Liverpool used Gemini to introduce new signing Bradley Barcola and to quiz fans, and recalled a win over Brentford with linked YouTube clips. Barcelona's October fixture preview passed 482K likes, and its players now ask Gemini a question during YouTube membership Q&As.

For teams selling or activating AI partnerships, the takeaway is to build content around something fans genuinely want, such as previews, new-signing explainers, quizzes or club history, and show the sponsor's tool delivering it. Visual hierarchy and data graphics beat walls of text. Recurring Stories with interactive stickers can be predictable sponsorship inventory because they mostly reach existing followers. Folding a partner into an established series, rather than launching a new one, keeps the activation natural.
