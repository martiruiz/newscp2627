Pat McAfee Brings Fan Punt-Catching Contest to ESPN's Monday Night Countdown

Social News · 17 November 2026 · Source: Front Office Sports

Pat McAfee and Jason Kelce staged a "Super Catch" contest on the field before the Ravens-Chargers Monday Night Football game in Baltimore. Army veteran and Ravens fan Richard Nelson, wearing a helmet and pads, caught a roughly 50-yard punt fired from a JUGS machine and won a ticket to Super Bowl LXI, which ESPN will broadcast for the first time on February 14.

It was the second such contest this season, following an October debut in Philadelphia where Kelce caught the punt and lateralled it to an Eagles fan. The bit spins off McAfee's "Kicking is Easy" segment on College GameDay, which has awarded $12 million to students and charities this season. ESPN says the contest will return occasionally rather than weekly.

For sports social teams, this is a reminder that simple, high-stakes fan challenges produce shareable moments with built-in drama, celebrity reactions and an emotional payoff. Clubs can run halftime or pregame skill contests tied to a big prize, film them vertically for TikTok and Reels, and use them to promote marquee events or partners while putting real supporters at the center of the story.

Why sports organizations should align content and partnerships teams to grow sponsorship revenue

In his Sponcon Sports newsletter, consultant Alex Kopilow argues that the most neglected relationship in sports organizations is the one between content and partnerships teams. Drawing on his consulting work and time at the Chicago White Sox, he says sellers often pitch formats content teams cannot deliver, while partnerships staff miss sellable ideas because they never hear what content is planning.

He proposes six changes for 2027: cross-attending each other's weekly meetings, bringing content staff into pitches and discovery sessions, building a shared digital inventory, sharing pitch updates and deal values, educating each other on goals and metrics, and giving the wider marketing department a shared digital sponsorship revenue target with a team bonus, provided audience and content standards are maintained.

SPORTS MARKETING 19 November 2026 SocialGeek News

Twitch brings back Hosting and tests shoppable ads at TwitchCon

At TwitchCon, Twitch announced that Hosting is returning, with tests starting at the end of the year: an offline channel can show another live stream with shared chat, and 'Host Trains' rotate audiences across chosen channels. Streamers will also be able to invite up to seven viewers into Luna games using their phones as controllers, and a new apprentice moderator role can time out users but not ban them.

On the business side, shoppable ads are in desktop beta for US and Canadian advertisers, letting viewers add products to a cart mid-stream. A skippable ad format enters open beta in December, with international shopping planned for early 2027. Twitch says creators landing sponsorships have quadrupled in a year, while total payouts have doubled.

SPORTS MARKETING 17 November 2026 Front Office Sports

Ryan Clark expands The Pivot to Fridays as athletes build their own media platforms

Former NFL player Ryan Clark, host of The Pivot, sat down with Dan Roberts of Front Office Sports for a video interview. Clark said the show is adding a Friday edition because of audience demand. He also discussed why more athletes are becoming hosts and analysts on their own platforms, how he now feels about leaving ESPN, Patrick Mahomes' standing as the face of the NFL, and the shake-up in the league's top broadcast booth.

For sports social teams, Clark's story shows that former and current athletes can build loyal audiences outside traditional broadcasting, with fan demand strong enough to justify more episodes. Clubs and leagues increasingly compete with, and can partner with, these athlete-led shows for attention, interviews and distribution.
