Instagram tests Features, horizontal videos up to 90 minutes linked from Reels

Social News · 5 November 2026 · Source: SocialGeek News

Instagram is trialling a format called Features that lets creators publish horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes, aimed at podcasts, interviews and entertainment shows. Reels can link straight to the full episode, and a Clips as Reels option cuts segments from a Feature into Reels. Features can surface in Explore and recommendations, and Instagram wants to bring them to TV screens.

The test is limited to a group of US creators after an earlier trial in Brazil. Monetization remains unclear: unlike YouTube, Instagram has not announced any ad revenue sharing for the format yet, and the move is widely seen as a direct challenge to YouTube's long-form dominance.

For clubs and leagues already producing podcasts, documentaries, press conferences or long interviews, this opens a second home for long-form content where their biggest audiences already scroll. Teams could cut highlight Reels from a full player interview or matchday show and drive fans to the complete episode without leaving the app. Those without long formats should start planning content that holds attention beyond 30 seconds.

Meta brings Edits video app to iPad with copyright checks and AI assistant upgrades

Meta has released an iPad version of Edits, its short-form video editing app, giving tablet users a larger composition screen with more tools visible at once. The launch follows the first desktop version for MacOS earlier this month. Alongside it, Meta added trim-upon-import, new filters, 1,600 new stickers and is testing LUT file imports for consistent color grading.

The Edits AI assistant can now take uploaded images and video and suggest how to build a video from them. A new copyright check on iOS also scans projects for potential rights issues before upload, with five checks available per day according to app researcher Radu Oncescu, helping creators avoid restrictions or penalties.

INSTAGRAM 19 November 2026 SocialGeek News

Instagram lets creators tag Amazon affiliate products directly in Reels and feed posts

Instagram now allows creators to tag Amazon products with affiliate links inside their content, up to 30 products per Reel and 20 per feed post. Once a creator connects an Amazon account through the Professional Dashboard, affiliate attribution is added automatically. Instagram takes no cut; Amazon continues paying creators for qualifying purchases.

For sports, this cuts the friction of the old 'link in bio' approach. Athletes can tag the boots, recovery gear or training equipment they actually use in a workout Reel, and fans can buy without leaving the video. Recommendation content becomes a direct revenue line for athletes and creators.

INSTAGRAM 16 November 2026 Social Media Today

Instagram launches dedicated app for Apple's foldable iPhone Duo with side-by-side Reels

Instagram has released a version of its app built specifically for the iPhone Duo, using the device's larger unfolded display. The main feed opens in a wider format, Reels show the video and its comments next to each other while playback continues, control buttons move to a sidebar, and messages open in a two-column layout. When the phone is folded, Stories and Reels keep their usual vertical format.

For sports social teams, the side-by-side Reels view puts the comment section on equal footing with the clip itself. That makes it worth seeding conversation early: pinned replies from the club account, prompts asking fans to predict a result or rate a goal, and quick responses to top comments can all become part of the viewing experience rather than an afterthought.
