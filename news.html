INSTAGRAM 8 Jun 2026

Be the commentator from home: X launches reaction videos to analyze goals and VAR

The platform X has launched “React with Video,” a tool inspired by the success of TikTok and Instagram Reels that lets users record themselves reacting to a post and publish it alongside the original clip.

For sports accounts this opens a new space for fan commentary: goals, VAR decisions and key moments can now be analyzed in video by creators, clubs and fans in real time.

FACEBOOK 8 Jun 2026

Meta launches paid versions: How will it impact sports on social media?

Meta announces that subscriptions on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp will offer extra features for users who pay a monthly fee, while the free versions remain available.

The biggest unknown for sports clubs is reach: if paid features change how content is shown, teams will need to rethink formats and distribution to keep engaging their audiences.
