SPORTS MARKETING 5 November 2026 SponCon Sports

MLB postseason lifts social engagement per post by 51%, reshaping sponsorship pricing

An analysis by SponCon Sports and Mondo Metrics of 57,239 posts from all 12 MLB postseason clubs found engagements per post rose 51% and views per post 26% versus the regular season. Even after adjusting for teams shifting more content to Instagram and TikTok, engagement still climbed 32%. Facebook showed the largest jump, with engagements up 79%, while World Series clubs averaged a 168% lift compared with 13% for Wild Card exits.

The takeaway for partnership and social teams is that playoff inventory deserves its own pricing, but not a blanket markup. The author recommends each club calculate its own postseason bump by platform and, ideally, by content series, then refresh those benchmarks every year it qualifies, since algorithms and fan behavior shift. Teams without recent playoff data can use the MLB findings as a starting reference.

YOUTUBE 5 November 2026 SocialGeek News

Study finds citing sources in YouTube descriptions boosts trust and sharing

A new study shows that YouTube creators who list their sources in video descriptions earn more viewer trust and make audiences more willing to share their content. It also found that adopting newsroom habits, such as citing, adding context and correcting errors, improves how trustworthy news videos are perceived, a lesson that extends beyond journalists to brands and creators.

Sports channels publishing explainers, tactical analysis, stats breakdowns or transfer and injury updates can apply this directly. Linking official data providers, league statements or original interviews in descriptions helps clubs and athletes stand out from rumor accounts, protect credibility and potentially increase shares among fans who want reliable information.

YOUTUBE 5 November 2026 SocialGeek News

YouTube tests lowering creator payout threshold from 100 to 20 dollars

YouTube is testing a reduction of the minimum earnings creators must reach before getting paid, cutting it from 100 to 20 dollars. The change would help smaller channels that currently wait months to hit the threshold and receive any income, and is read as an effort to attract and keep emerging creators as Instagram moves into long-form video.

For sports organizations, faster payouts could encourage more niche fan creators, ex-players and grassroots channels to professionalize. That widens the pool of smaller, highly engaged creators that clubs and sponsors can partner with for targeted campaigns, such as academy content, women's teams coverage or local fan-led shows, often at lower cost than big names.

INSTAGRAM 5 November 2026 SocialGeek News

Instagram tests Features, horizontal videos up to 90 minutes linked from Reels

Instagram is trialling a format called Features that lets creators publish horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes, aimed at podcasts, interviews and entertainment shows. Reels can link straight to the full episode, and a Clips as Reels option cuts segments from a Feature into Reels. Features can surface in Explore and recommendations, and Instagram wants to bring them to TV screens.

The test is limited to a group of US creators after an earlier trial in Brazil. Monetization remains unclear: unlike YouTube, Instagram has not announced any ad revenue sharing for the format yet, and the move is widely seen as a direct challenge to YouTube's long-form dominance.

SNAPCHAT 5 November 2026 Social Media Today

Snapchat user growth stalls in North America and Europe while Spotlight surges

Snap's Q3 2026 results show Snapchat reaching 495 million daily active users, just 2 million more than the previous quarter, with zero growth in North America and Europe. Revenue still rose 14% year over year to $1.72 billion. Teen social media bans are a growing risk: in Australia, Snapchat locked or disabled more than 415,000 accounts belonging to under-16 users.

The bright spot is short-form video. Snap says the number of people posting to Spotlight grew 130% year over year and daily active viewers rose 40%, while messaging and two-way communication also climbed. Subscriptions across Snapchat+, Lens+ and Memories Storage passed 30 million, and Snap is expanding creator partnership options for brands.

SPORTS MARKETING 4 November 2026 Front Office Sports

High school athletic directors now run content operations as NIL and short-form video reshape prep sports

Front Office Sports spoke with around a dozen veteran high school and state athletic administrators across the U.S., who say the job has become far more demanding over the past five years. They link the shift to the arrival of NIL alongside the boom in short-form video. ADs like Ponte Vedra's Mike Harrison now sell videoboard ads, produce hype videos for big games and manage promotion of teams and individual athletes on school social channels.

Families increasingly expect instant, individualized exposure. Sidelines are filling with private content creators hired by athletes, and one Ohio AD said programs without highlight reels posted within an hour of a game are seen as falling behind. To keep up, many schools are recruiting student volunteers interested in tech and journalism to capture and share game content. Actual NIL deals remain rare, but ADs expect compliance work to arrive soon.

SPORTS MARKETING 3 November 2026 Social Media Today

Reddit begins testing query-targeted ads inside its in-app search results

Reddit has started testing ads in the search results of its app. The ads are targeted by search query, carry a small ad label at the top of the results panel, and are placed mainly on contextual relevance. The test is open first to a select group of eligible advertisers. Reddit says search pages show strong purchase and search intent, and that half of its users credit the platform with helping them discover something new.

The move comes as Reddit pushes discovery. A July study found that 72% of its users come looking for information and 64% arrive with a specific goal. However, CEO Steve Huffman said in the Q3 update that search referrals fell by more than a million per week on average in October as AI-driven traffic declined, which could affect the value of its discovery features.

LINKEDIN 3 November 2026 Social Media Today

LinkedIn adds post performance table and new saves, sends and network metrics

LinkedIn has explained how its refreshed creator analytics will work, following last month's broader creator tools update. A new Post Performance Table lists every post alongside its impressions, views and watch time, so users can compare content without opening each post's analytics separately. LinkedIn's content product director Sam Corrao Clannon outlined the changes, adding that the company will watch how creators use the data before deciding on further improvements.

Several metrics have also been expanded. Creators can now see which specific members followed them from a given post, how many people saved or sent a post to someone else, and how many views came from connections versus people outside their network. LinkedIn says it is also working on new monetization options for creators as it pushes for more feed engagement.

TIKTOK 2 November 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok formalizes WTA partnership with exclusive livestreams and a new Creator Club

TikTok has signed a formal content deal with the Women's Tennis Association after a year of collaboration that pushed the WTA account past one million followers. WTA content generated a combined 250 million views and engagements over the past year, up 41% year over year. The first activation is an exclusive TikTok livestream of the WTA Finals Draw Ceremony on November 3, ahead of the season-ending event in Indian Wells.

The agreement also launches a WTA Creator Club, giving digital creators access to tournaments worldwide to film behind-the-scenes and off-court stories. The WTA will also gain tournament content hubs through TikTok's GamePlan offering, and both partners say player-led storytelling stays central, with support for athletes building their own presence on the app. TikTok signed a similar deal with the ATP last year.

SPORTS MARKETING 2 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Internet Invitational Shows How Creator Golf Turns Sponsors Into Part of the Show

The second Internet Invitational, the creator golf event from Barstool Sports and Bob Does Sports, has built its sponsorship model around brands that join the entertainment instead of interrupting it. Dunkin' returns as presenting sponsor with an on-course drive-thru, Zyrtec owns a branded Relief Hole, and Reese's backs a $500K hole-in-one challenge. With $4M at stake, pressure is part of the appeal.

WHOOP also brought back its WHOOP Live activation, first used at the Ryder Cup, which shows players' heart rates during tense moments. That turns biometric data into a storytelling tool that heightens drama for viewers while keeping the partner visibly tied to the action rather than placed in a separate ad break.
