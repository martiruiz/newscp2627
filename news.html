Aaron Rodgers' McAfee Show interview shows the power of athlete-friendly platforms

SPORTS MARKETING 25 November 2026 SponCon Sports

A 60-minute research method for building tailored sponsored content pitches

Sponcon Sports newsletter author Alex Kopilow has outlined a three-step process, collect, compare and customize, for creating brand-specific digital partnership ideas in about an hour. It draws on eight free resources, including secondary social accounts that follow every pro team and league, sports broadcasts as a source of segment names, X search filtered to people you follow, and Meta's Branded Content search and Ad Library.

He also suggests YouTube's Gemini-powered Ask feature to locate sponsor integrations within videos, checking a partner's website, bios and search ads for taglines and priority products, and an AI prompt that finds category terms with double meanings in a given sport, such as linking tire brands to a baseball pitching rotation feature.

AI 24 November 2026 Social Media Today

Emplifi survey: AI boosts social team output without shrinking headcount

Emplifi polled 405 marketing managers, directors and executives about trends shaping their plans for 2027. Ninety percent said AI has raised their team's output, yet 54% reported their social media team actually grew in size rather than shrinking. Sixty-nine percent said AI tools save them at least three hours each week on reporting, and 55% expect short-form video to keep gaining relevance and influence.

For club, league and athlete social teams, the data supports treating AI as a capacity multiplier rather than a replacement. Automating routine work such as weekly performance reports, sponsor recaps and post-match analytics can free hours for creative tasks like matchday storytelling, player-led content and fan interaction. That reclaimed time is also a useful argument when making the case for team investment to leadership.

Kalshi sponsors 49ers WAGs' YouTube series to reach female NFL fans

SPORTS MARKETING 23 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Dr Pepper reaches college football fans through House of Highlights' CLASH debate series

Dr Pepper has found a way into college football conversations without partnering with a team or league. The brand worked with CLASH, the debate and trivia channel from House of Highlights, on three episodes featuring coach Ed Orgeron, creator MMG and former Heisman winner Troy Smith. Alongside product placement and branded messaging, the activation added merchandise QR codes and paid ads running against the videos, pushing views past 2 million.

The lesson for sports marketers is that fan attention increasingly sits with media brands and creators, not only official rights holders. X 22 November 2026 Social Media Today

X adds custom video thumbnails, new caption styles and one-tap vertical formatting

X has rolled out a new set of video creation tools: more caption styles and colours, the ability to manually pick a thumbnail frame from an uploaded clip, extra text overlays, and layout options that convert horizontal footage to vertical in one tap using Fill, Fade or Stack. FACEBOOK 19 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta tests Achados, a Brazil-only shopping app merging Marketplace, Groups and Instagram listings

Meta has released Achados, an experimental shopping app currently available only on Android in Brazil. It pulls items for sale from Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Groups and Instagram sellers into a single local feed. It adds price comparisons against similar products across Meta's apps, plus seller profiles, customer reviews and sales histories. It is one of several new apps Meta has launched this year.

For sports organisations, the signal is Meta's push to treat Instagram and Facebook posts as shoppable inventory. Clubs, fan shops and sports brands in Brazil that already sell merchandise, tickets-adjacent products or matchday gear through Instagram or Facebook could gain extra visibility without building new storefronts. Because listings sit next to price comparisons, consistent pricing and clear product posts become more important.
