YOUTUBE 24 November 2026 Front Office Sports

Dan Rapaport discusses the Internet Invitational and where YouTube golf goes next

Scratch Golf's Dan Rapaport joined Front Office Sports' David Rumsey for a video conversation on golf's evolving media landscape. They covered his role calling the Skins Game on Prime Video on Black Friday, why players have to carry a made-for-TV format, whether Tiger Woods is now a special-event golfer, and the future of the Internet Invitational and the broader YouTube golf scene.

For sports social teams, the discussion shows how creator-led competitions on YouTube now sit alongside streaming-platform specials as serious golf properties. Partnering with established golf creators or building collaborative YouTube formats can reach younger fans who follow the sport through creators rather than traditional broadcasts.

YOUTUBE 24 November 2026 Front Office Sports

Kalshi sponsors 49ers WAGs' YouTube series to reach female NFL fans

Prediction market Kalshi has partnered with Wife'd Up, Mic'd Up, the YouTube series hosted by 49ers spouses Kristin Juszczyk and Claire Kittle. The six-episode deal features a guest partner of another NFL player in each installment, plus a branded prediction segment tied to her gameday. The first episode, with Haley Cavinder, passed 130,000 views, and launch videos on Instagram drew nearly 700,000 views combined.

The integration is built natively into creator formats: a get-ready-with-me announcement, onscreen QR codes, sign-up links in descriptions and segments woven into real travel and gameday stories. Kalshi says the goal is reaching new audiences, mainly women, whose presence on the platform it says grew about 60% in a year. The NFL bans players from endorsing prediction markets, raising questions since Kyle Juszczyk appears in the content.

INSTAGRAM 23 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta brings Edits video app to iPad with copyright checks and AI assistant upgrades

Meta has released an iPad version of Edits, its short-form video editing app, giving tablet users a larger composition screen with more tools visible at once. The launch follows the first desktop version for MacOS earlier this month. SPORTS MARKETING 23 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Dr Pepper reaches college football fans through House of Highlights' CLASH debate series

Dr Pepper has found a way into college football conversations without partnering with a team or league. The brand worked with CLASH, the debate and trivia channel from House of Highlights, on three episodes featuring coach Ed Orgeron, creator MMG and former Heisman winner Troy Smith. X adds custom video thumbnails, new caption styles and one-tap vertical formatting

X has rolled out a new set of video creation tools: more caption styles and colours, the ability to manually pick a thumbnail frame from an uploaded clip, extra text overlays, and layout options that convert horizontal footage to vertical in one tap using Fill, Fade or Stack. FACEBOOK 19 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta tests Achados, a Brazil-only shopping app merging Marketplace, Groups and Instagram listings

Meta has released Achados, an experimental shopping app currently available only on Android in Brazil. THREADS 19 November 2026 Social Media Today

Threads tests upvoted Q&A sessions inside live chats

Threads is trialling a Q&A format within its live chats. Hosts can collect questions from participants, and the chat then votes on which ones should be asked, creating a more structured, interview-style session. The approach resembles Reddit's AMA model, where the community steers the conversation by upvoting. The test is running in a limited number of live chat sessions, though every participant can read the answers.

The feature builds on live chat, which Threads launched in April and expanded in September, as Meta pushes the app toward real-time conversation around major events. X still leads for live event discussion, but Threads is gaining ground and wants to be a stronger option for communities and brands looking to engage audiences in the moment.

SPORTS MARKETING 19 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Why sports organizations should align content and partnerships teams to grow sponsorship revenue

In his Sponcon Sports newsletter, consultant Alex Kopilow argues that the most neglected relationship in sports organizations is the one between content and partnerships teams. Drawing on his consulting work and time at the Chicago White Sox, he says sellers often pitch formats content teams cannot deliver, while partnerships staff miss sellable ideas because they never hear what content is planning.

He proposes six changes for 2027: cross-attending each other's weekly meetings, bringing content staff into pitches and discovery sessions, building a shared digital inventory, sharing pitch updates and deal values, educating each other on goals and metrics, and giving the wider marketing department a shared digital sponsorship revenue target with a team bonus, provided audience and content standards are maintained.
