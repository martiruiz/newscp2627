NFL, Commanders and ESPN use Instagram collabs to hype Sam Hartman's Bachelor casting

Washington Commanders practice squad quarterback Sam Hartman was announced as the lead for the 30th season of ABC's The Bachelor. Right after the reveal on Good Morning America, the NFL, the Commanders and ESPN published Instagram collaboration posts with ABC and the show's official account, plus similar TikTok content. ESPN also plans to keep coordinating with Disney sister brands on Bachelor content relevant to its fans. Hartman's former college programs, Wake Forest and Notre Dame, joined in as well.

INSTAGRAM 12 November 2026 Social Media Today

Meta's Edits app adds speed curves, auto audio balancing and text timing controls

Meta has rolled out a new batch of features for Edits, its video editing app tied to Instagram. Creators can now apply speed curves to vary playback speed within a clip, choosing from presets or building their own. An auto-adjust tool balances audio levels across video elements, and new controls let users set how long text animations stay on screen and how fast they loop.

Edits also now lets users pick which topics they want to see more or less of in its Inspiration tab, similar to Meta's Your Algorithm tool for feeds. The update continues Meta's near-biweekly release cadence for the app, which has also recently arrived on desktop, giving editors a larger workspace.

INSTAGRAM 12 November 2026 Social Media Today

Instagram tests feature letting users pick favorite sports and leagues for updates

Instagram appears to be testing a sports preferences option, spotted by app researcher Alessandro Paluzzi. Users would choose from a list of major sports and tailor which league updates they receive. Instagram would then use those choices to surface real-time sports updates and show more sports content to interested users. The move follows Threads adding sports-focused elements to capture live event discussion. Instagram has not commented on the test.

For clubs and leagues, this could mean that fans who declare an interest in a sport or league are more likely to be shown related posts, including live moments. If the feature launches, teams should lean into timely, real-time content around matches, such as quick results graphics, goal clips and reaction Reels, so they are well placed to benefit from any boost in sports-focused distribution.

YOUTUBE 12 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Mercedes F1's Antonelli title interview shows how YouTube packaging makes or breaks sponsored content

After Kimi Antonelli became Formula 1's youngest World Champion, Mercedes released an exclusive interview on its Nu-sponsored video podcast. Despite a channel of over 2M subscribers, the 30-minute episode drew about 30K views and nine comments in six days. Its title led with the series name and episode number, and the thumbnail was crowded with show branding and Nu's logo. F1's own interview, framed clearly as the champion's first interview, reached 935K views in a week.

YouTube strategist Gen Kimura argued Mercedes did not lead with value: YouTube is intent-driven, so viewers need a reason to click. He also called subscriber counts a vanity metric, saying brands increasingly look at a channel's consistent view floor. Meanwhile, Mercedes' Instagram announcement and clips generated 2.9M views and over 727K engagements, a reminder to measure sponsor value across the whole content package.

SPORTS MARKETING 12 November 2026 SocialGeek News

EU draft rules would require influencers to clearly label sponsored content

The European Union's draft Digital Fairness Act seeks to make influencers clearly label sponsored content. The proposal is also expected to include measures to limit compulsive use of online platforms.

Sports organisations that pay athletes, ambassadors or creators to promote partners should review their disclosure practices before the rules take shape. Clubs activating sponsorships through player accounts or creator collaborations will need clear, consistent ad labels in contracts and briefs, which also protects fan trust. Planning compliant formats now avoids last-minute changes to campaigns across EU markets.

Instagram tests Features, horizontal videos up to 90 minutes, to rival YouTube

Instagram is testing a format called Features that lets creators upload horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes, aimed at podcasts, interviews and entertainment shows. LINKEDIN 11 November 2026 Social Media Today

LinkedIn tests showing how often each person revisits your profile

LinkedIn is trialling a frequent viewer indicator for Premium subscribers, according to a Wired report. Beyond the existing list of who viewed a profile, the feature would show how many times a specific person returned to that page within a set period. X 10 November 2026 Social Media Today

X launches video clipping so users can reshare credited snippets of longer videos

X has introduced a clipping option for videos lasting two minutes or more. From the repost menu, users can select a segment between 5 and 60 seconds, capped at 30% of the original, and share it with their own commentary. The clip carries an overlay with the creator's handle, a timestamp showing where it sits in the full video, and a tap-through link to the original.

Both the original poster and the account sharing the clip are credited with resulting impressions, so each can earn through X's Original Creator Rewards program. Creators currently cannot disable clipping; the only way to remove unwanted clips is deleting the original post. Live broadcasts are not yet supported. The feature is live on iOS and Android, with web access to follow.

YOUTUBE 10 November 2026 Social Media Today

YouTube survey of 20,000 EU creators highlights demand for studios, support and algorithm transparency

YouTube has released video interviews with European creators that expand on its recent creator consultation, which surveyed 20,000 creators across all 27 EU member states. The initial findings, published in a 17-page Creative Capital report, list five main concerns: more government recognition and support, closer collaboration with traditional industries, more investment in skills training, better access to physical production space such as studios, and clearer information on how recommendation algorithms affect reach.

YouTube is using the research to engage policymakers. It says it contributed more than $9.3 billion to the European economy in 2025 and supported over 220,000 jobs. For sports organisations, the findings show where creators feel underserved, and those gaps line up with assets many clubs and leagues already have: production facilities, access, and an established traditional media business.

TIKTOK 9 November 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok upgrades Symphony AI ad tools with latest Dreamina Seedance video model

TikTok has expanded its Symphony AI ad creation suite. The Symphony Agent now taps the newest Dreamina Seedance models, letting advertisers upload up to 50 multimodal references (images, video and audio) instead of 9, and generate clips of up to 30 seconds per prompt, double the earlier 15-second cap. TikTok says output is sharper, with better lighting and more consistent characters across frames.

TikTok also released an updated Symphony API so advertisers can run Symphony generation inside their own creative management tools. In addition, starting November 9, advertisers outside North America can buy BytePlus tokens to access Seedance through Symphony. BytePlus is the enterprise tech arm of parent company ByteDance and runs its AI systems.
