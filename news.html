TikTok launches brand safety center with new counterfeit and copyright protection tools

TikTok has rolled out a new brand safety center website that gathers its guidance for marketers in one place, including videos with internal experts, a new Brand Protection Playbook and an AI Safety Essentials overview. Meta's creator assistant adds trend alerts, storyboard help and AI comment replies

Meta has expanded its AI-powered creator assistant, first launched in June to guide creators on Facebook and Instagram. The tool can now proactively flag trends and opportunities relevant to a creator's niche, turn an idea into a storyboard and shot list, and review a draft before publishing with specific feedback. TIKTOK 2 November 2026 Social Media Today

TikTok formalizes WTA partnership with exclusive livestreams and a new Creator Club

TikTok has signed a formal content deal with the Women's Tennis Association after a year of collaboration that pushed the WTA account past one million followers. WTA content generated a combined 250 million views and engagements over the past year, up 41% year over year. The first activation is an exclusive TikTok livestream of the WTA Finals Draw Ceremony on November 3, ahead of the season-ending event in Indian Wells.

The agreement also launches a WTA Creator Club, giving digital creators access to tournaments worldwide to film behind-the-scenes and off-court stories. The WTA will also gain tournament content hubs through TikTok's GamePlan offering, and both partners say player-led storytelling stays central, with support for athletes building their own presence on the app. TikTok signed a similar deal with the ATP last year.

SPORTS MARKETING 2 November 2026 SponCon Sports

Internet Invitational Shows How Creator Golf Turns Sponsors Into Part of the Show

The second Internet Invitational, the creator golf event from Barstool Sports and Bob Does Sports, has built its sponsorship model around brands that join the entertainment instead of interrupting it. X 1 November 2026 Social Media Today

X launches invite-only Video Kickstart bonus paying creators for regular vertical video

X has introduced Video Kickstart, an invite-only pilot that pays creators a bonus for posting original short-form vertical videos. To qualify in a roughly month-long period, participants must publish 15 eligible clips on 15 separate days, each under three minutes. Content first shared on other platforms is allowed as long as the creator owns it, and enrollees are fast-tracked into X's ad revenue sharing program.

The move continues X's push into video, following a revamped livestream studio, new overlay options and an updated video composer. For sports social teams, it signals that X will likely reward native vertical video with more visibility, making it worth repurposing owned clips from TikTok, Reels or Shorts rather than only posting links and text updates.

THREADS 29 October 2026 Social Media Today

Threads tests Threads Friends, a Close Friends-style list for private posting

Threads is trialling Threads Friends, a feature that lets users build a private list of up to 50 connections and publish posts visible only to that group. It works much like Instagram's Close Friends: people added can see they are on the list, and members can view each other's likes and replies on those posts. The test is running in Ireland, Italy and Japan, though anyone worldwide can be added to a list.

For sports social teams, this points to a broader shift from public broadcasting toward smaller, curated sharing spaces. Athletes could use a Friends list to share behind-the-scenes thoughts with family, teammates or a handful of trusted creators, while clubs might reserve it for media partners, ambassadors or a select group of superfans.

SPORTS MARKETING 29 October 2026 Front Office Sports

PGA Tour Lands Short-Term Sponsor for Austin Event After Good Good Golf Exit

The PGA Tour has found a partial replacement for Good Good Golf, the creator group that withdrew from sponsoring and hosting a new Austin tournament after backlash over a controversial Callaway driver ad. Compliance Solutions, a Florida tax services firm already backing a Korn Ferry Tour event, will become presenting sponsor of the renamed Austin Championship for this year only, paying less than the $6 million Good Good had committed.

The reduced fee reflects how little time remains to activate: practice rounds start in about 11 days, leaving the sponsor almost no runway for promotion. Meanwhile, Good Good has also lost its Callaway deal, its Golf Channel show and retail placement for its merchandise, showing how quickly a creator brand's commercial ecosystem can unravel after a misstep.

SPORTS MARKETING 29 October 2026 SponCon Sports

NBA opening week sponsored content report: what top teams did with partners

A Sponcon Sports analysis with Mondo Metrics reviewed 11,919 posts from all 30 NBA teams across five platforms during opening week. About 11.7% carried a brand integration, producing 5.6M engagements and at least 42M views for 193 brands. The 76ers led sponsored engagement, Golden State dominated views thanks to a Steph Curry warmup trick shot backed by IREN, and Michelob ULTRA was the most featured sponsor, shifting toward a Courtside Moments series.

Standout ideas include jersey patch partners running surveys and uniform votes to gather first-party data, the Sixers' Bloom Energy 'Power Run' giveaway pushed via Instagram Stories, and series names that link naturally to the sponsor, such as Hornets x Lowe's 'Drilling Threes'. Swapping 'presented by' for phrases like 'Delivered By FedEx' and embedding promo codes in win graphics also added brand meaning without standalone ads.

FACEBOOK 29 October 2026 SocialGeek News

Facebook tests charging Pages to post links beyond a small monthly quota

Facebook is testing a cap on how many link posts a Page can publish. Some Pages are now limited to two link posts per month, after which a prompt invites them to subscribe through Meta One: $50 a month for eight links, $150 for 20, or $500 for unlimited. Links to compatible affiliates, Creator Storefront and Meta products remain unrestricted. Meta calls it a test, though it has reportedly reached nonprofit and library Pages that were meant to be exempt.

For clubs and leagues that push fans from Facebook to ticketing, shop or news pages, this signals that outbound traffic is getting more expensive. Social teams should audit how much web traffic Facebook really drives and reserve link posts for high-value moments like ticket drops or kit launches.

Meta credits AI ad ranking upgrades for higher clicks and conversions on Facebook

Meta has outlined how AI improvements to its ad delivery are lifting results. The company said ranking upgrades in Q2 2026 brought 8.3% more ad clicks and a 15.7% rise in conversions on Facebook.
