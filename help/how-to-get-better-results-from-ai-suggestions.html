How to get better results from AI-generated suggestions

AI ToolsUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

In Sports Content Playbook the AI suggestions are only as useful as the post you analyse. Pick a post that matches your sport, format and resources, check "How it performed" and "How to adapt it to your club", then rewrite the Hook ideas, Shot list and Caption draft with your club's real facts and voice.

How the suggestions are made

Each AI analysis in Sports Content Playbook is built from one real post. For videos (reels, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) the AI watches frames of the whole video and reads an automatic transcript; if the video can't be read, it falls back to the cover. The How it performed verdict is calculated from the post's real numbers against its own account's median that quarter. The AI never invents numbers and never identifies people by their face.

There is no prompt to tweak and no chat to ask follow-up questions. That makes the post you choose the main lever: a good match gives you sports content ideas you can actually shoot; a poor match gives you suggestions you'll have to throw away.

Step by step: choose posts that give better suggestions

  1. In the Content Library, narrow the results first: Sport, Competition, Format and Platform, plus the Match Day timeline if it's for a game.
  2. Prefer Overperforming posts (at least 2x their own account's median) and Replicable ideas (easy to copy with your own assets).
  3. Try a sentence in the search box, such as "transfer reels with a sponsor that worked". It turns words into filters and doesn't use any AI analyses.
  4. Before unlocking, open the post and read Tags and caption, and check More like this for a closer match.
  5. Click Unlock AI Analysis. Read How it performed: "Too early to judge" or "No numbers yet" means the result isn't proven yet.
  6. Read How to adapt it to your club. If the AI says Hard to replicate, keep the hook idea and look for another post for the format.

Practical example: a basketball academy without a video team

A basketball academy has one coach who films on a phone. Instead of analysing big-club productions, the coach filters Replicable ideas and Challenges, Sport basketball, Platform TikTok, and finds two short challenge videos with young players. She unlocks both analyses, compares the 3 Hook ideas of each, keeps the clearest one and uses the shorter Shot list. The result is a set of sports TikTok content ideas the academy can film in one training session, with a caption she rewrote in the academy's tone.

Common mistakes

  • Analysing posts that don't match your reality. A suggestion built on a stadium-wide production won't fit a club with no camera operator.
  • Publishing the Caption draft as it is. It has [placeholders] on purpose; fill them with real dates, names and scores.
  • Ignoring the verdict. A post "Below its usual" can still teach a hook, but don't copy its whole approach.
  • Spending the allowance in the first week. Starter has 5 analyses a month, Plus 10 and Pro 30; unused ones don't carry over. Packs never expire and are used after the monthly ones.
  • Unlocking what a teammate already analysed. On team plans, check the My team's tab in My AI analyses first: reading their analyses is free.

Keep what works

A sports social media strategy improves when you reuse what worked. Use Download PDF on an analysis to share it, save the original post to a board in Saved boards, and on Plus and Pro drag the analysed post to your Content Plan: the AI's adaptation goes into the entry's notes. Used like this, SCP is one of the AI tools for sports content creation that gets more useful the more carefully you pick. To write the final copy, see how to create captions, hooks and scripts with AI.

Frequently asked questions

Can I ask the AI to change a suggestion?

No. There is no AI chat or prompt box. Analyse a different post that fits better, or edit the hook, shot list and caption draft yourself.

Why does the analysis say "Too early to judge"?

The post's numbers were collected less than 48 hours after it was published, so SCP doesn't compare it with the account's median yet.

What happens if the AI can't read a video?

It uses the post's cover instead, so the visual breakdown is based on that image rather than on frames of the video.

What if I run out of AI analyses?

Your allowance renews monthly from your subscription date. You can also buy one-off packs in Profile → Billing & membership → Buy more AI analyses.
