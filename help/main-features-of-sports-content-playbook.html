What are the main features of Sports Content Playbook?

Getting StartedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Sports Content Playbook has three main tools: the Content Library (500,000+ real sports posts with smart filters, every plan), AI analysis (why a post worked and how to adapt it, every plan) and the Content Plan (a week and month calendar, Plus and Pro). Enterprise clubs also get My Club, a private library of their own accounts.

Content Library

The Content Library is the core of Sports Content Playbook and what sets it apart from generic sports content creation tools: more than 500,000 posts from 770+ clubs and 110+ competitions in 17 sports, on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Facebook, with new posts every day. Available in every plan.

  • Filters grouped in What works, Match Day, Brands & sponsors, Where & how and Inspiration from. AI applies these labels to every post.
  • Search: type a word ("signings", a club, "reels") or a whole sentence such as "transfer reels with a sponsor that worked"; it turns your words into filters for free.
  • Special selections: Top Picks (chosen by the SCP team), Replicable ideas, Overperforming and Challenges.
  • Saved boards: private boards to keep the posts you like, plus Saved searches and shareable search links.
  • Public link on each post, so anyone can open that single post without an account.

AI analysis

On any post, Unlock AI Analysis gives you a structured breakdown: Summary, Hook (with a reusable template), Why it works, How it performed, Format, Angle, a Visual breakdown or Slide by slide, Scroll stoppers, How to adapt it to your club and a Ready to use block with 3 hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft. For videos, the AI watches frames of the whole video and reads a transcript of what is said.

Each plan includes a monthly allowance: Starter 5, Plus 10, Pro 30 (5 during the free trial). Reopening an analysis you already unlocked is free, and on team plans you can read your teammates' analyses for free. This is SCP's take on AI tools for sports content creation: it explains real posts rather than writing from nothing.

Content Plan

The Content Plan (Plus and Pro) is a sports content calendar with week and month views. You drag posts from the library onto a day, give each entry a status (Idea, Planned, In production, Published) and a type (Post, Carousel, Reel, Story, YouTube, TikTok), and add a caption and notes. It also includes a Weekly base for recurring posts, 12 key dates (from Pre-season to Christmas), Fill my week and exports to PDF, Excel or Calendar (.ics).

My Club (Enterprise)

My Club is a private space for a club, competition, agency or federation with posts from its own players, sponsors, teams and sections, plus competitors and content creators. SCP sets it up with you and uploads the posts every day; only your marketing and communications team sees it. It includes everything in Pro. Pricing is custom, from €99/month, explained in a 30-minute demo.

Which plan includes what

  • Starter: Content Library, search and smart filters, Match Day, Saved boards, 5 AI analyses a month.
  • Plus: everything in Starter plus the Content Creators filter, the Content Plan and 10 AI analyses a month.
  • Pro: everything in Plus plus the Brands & sponsors filters and 30 AI analyses a month.
  • Enterprise: My Club plus everything in Pro.

How the features fit together: an example

A handball federation's media officer wants a sponsor activation for the cup final. With Pro, he filters Brands & sponsors by sponsor sector, saves five posts to a board, unlocks an AI analysis of the strongest one and drags it to the Content Plan, where the AI's adaptation appears in the entry notes. He exports the week as Excel and sends it to his sponsor manager. As sports marketing tools go, that is the whole loop: find, understand, plan.

SCP does not publish posts, generate captions from a blank prompt or offer an AI chat. Its writing help is inside the AI analysis: the hook template, hook ideas, shot list and caption draft.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a shared team calendar?

No. Each user's Content Plan is private. Teams share work by exporting the plan (PDF, Excel or .ics), sharing saved-search links and public post links.

Can I buy more AI analyses?

Yes. One-off packs never expire and are used after your monthly allowance. Buy them in Profile → Billing & membership → Buy more AI analyses or on the pricing page.

Can I download other clubs' videos?

No. You can watch the embedded post, copy its caption and share a public link, but downloading the video or image is not possible.

What is the difference between a sports content calendar and the Content Plan?

The Content Plan is SCP's calendar inside the platform. SCP also offers a free Sports Content Calendar 2026-27 PDF with 28 key dates for 9 sports in Free resources.
