How to find trending content and creative inspiration

Content LibraryUpdated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

Sports Content Playbook has no single "trending" tab: to see what is working right now, filter the Content Library by a recent period (This week or This month), sort by Most viewed and add Overperforming, then narrow by platform and format. For platform news and sports social media trends, read Social News and the blog.

How SCP shows you what's trending

Sports Content Playbook tracks the best accounts in sports every day and adds new posts to the library daily: clubs, leagues and brands on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Facebook. A trend, in practice, is a format or idea that several accounts are using now and that is getting results. The library gives you three ways to see that:

  • Period: This week or This month to see only recent posts.
  • Sort: Most viewed to bring the biggest numbers to the top.
  • Overperforming: posts that got at least 2x the median of their own account in the same quarter, recomputed every hour. This is the best signal that something is working beyond the account's usual reach.

Step by step: what's working this week

  1. Open the Content Library.
  2. Set the period to This week.
  3. Change the sort to Most viewed.
  4. In Filters → What works, click Overperforming.
  5. In Where & how, choose a Platform (for example TikTok or Instagram) and a Format.
  6. Add your Sport from Inspiration from, or leave it open to see what works in other sports.
  7. Save the combination from Saved searches… so you can reopen it every Monday.

Ideas by platform

  • Sports TikTok content ideas: Platform: TikTok + Overperforming + This month. Add Challenges to see tests, games and contests with players, a format that is also Replicable.
  • Sports reels ideas: Platform: Instagram + Format: reels + Replicable ideas to keep only what you can make with your own assets.
  • Sports Instagram content ideas beyond video: Format: carousel and sort by Most viewed. The AI analysis of a carousel includes a slide-by-slide breakdown.
  • Content Creators (from Plus): formats from creators, filtered by creator type, language, country, gender and followers. Useful to see what creators do before clubs adopt it.

Creative inspiration when you're stuck

  • Sort by Surprise me to break your usual search habits.
  • Look outside your sport: remove the Sport filter and see what basketball, NFL or cycling accounts are doing with the same format.
  • Open a post you like and click More like this (same format, sport and competition).
  • Type top picks in the search box for the SCP team's editorial selection.
  • Outside the library: Social News covers daily platform updates for sports teams (Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube…), the blog publishes an article a day including sports social media trends, and the free weekly newsletter arrives every Monday.

Example: a basketball club's Monday

Every Monday, the content lead of a basketball club opens a saved search: This week, Most viewed, Overperforming, Platform: TikTok. She sees that three accounts in other sports posted the same kind of locker-room challenge and that all three beat their usual numbers. She opens one, runs the AI analysis, and drags it to Wednesday in her Content Plan. Then she clears the Platform filter, switches to Instagram and repeats.

Things to keep in mind

  • Metrics refresh when a post is played, at most every 6 hours, so this week's numbers are still moving.
  • A post counted less than 48 hours after publishing shows Too early to judge in its AI analysis.
  • Most viewed favours big accounts. Overperforming balances that by comparing each post with its own account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a trending section in SCP?

Not as a separate tab. You see what's trending by combining a recent period, the Most viewed sort and the Overperforming filter in the Content Library.

How fresh is the library?

New posts are added daily from the accounts SCP tracks, and the Overperforming selection is recomputed every hour.

Where can I read about platform changes?

In Social News, which publishes daily platform updates for sports teams, and in the blog's Social media trends articles.

Can I get trends without opening the library?

The free weekly newsletter arrives every Monday, and the blog publishes one article a day.
