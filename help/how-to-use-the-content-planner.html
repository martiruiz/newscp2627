How to use the Content Planner

Content PlannerRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

The Content Plan (Content Planner) is the calendar of Sports Content Playbook, included in Plus and Pro. You drag posts from the Content Library onto a day, write the caption and notes, set the type and status (Idea, Planned, In production, Published) and export the week or month as PDF, Excel or a calendar file.

What the Content Plan is

The Content Plan is a sports content planning tool inside Sports Content Playbook. It has a Week view (days × hours) and a Month view, a Today button and a New plan button to keep several plans apart (for example Pre-season, Kit launch or Women's team). Every change is saved automatically.

Each entry has a status (Idea, Planned, In production, Published), a type (Post, Carousel, Reel, Story, YouTube, TikTok), a Caption, Notes and, if it came from the library, the inspiration post with its original link. Giving an idea a date makes it Planned.

Step by step: plan your first week

  1. Open Content Plan and choose Week. Use New plan if you want a separate plan for a campaign.
  2. Set up your Weekly base: the recurring posts you publish every week (for example a Monday result graphic or a Thursday training video). They show as red placeholders until you plan them; click one to turn it into an entry.
  3. Add a post: click the + in a time slot, or use New idea in the No date tray for ideas without a day yet.
  4. Bring inspiration in: open Ideas & inspiration, open one of your saved boards and drag a post onto a day.
  5. Open the entry and fill in the type, Caption and Notes. Move the status forward as the post gets made.
  6. Click Download and choose PDF, Excel or Calendar (.ics for Google, Outlook, Apple). Tick Include "No date" ideas if you want them in the file.

Planning from the Content Library

You don't have to open the planner to fill it. In the Content Library, the Content Plan panel lets you:

  • Drag a post onto a day. Shift+click to pick several posts at once.
  • Use the + on a post to open a mini calendar, which also works on phones.
  • Use Fill my week to fill empty days with posts from your favourite profiles; the counter shows "n/7 days with content".

If you drag a post you already analysed with AI, the AI's adaptation goes into the entry's notes. The calendar also shows 12 key dates (such as Pre-season on 15 July, Fixture release on 15 August, Halloween or Black Friday); each opens the library filtered to that topic, so you find sports content ideas for it in one click.

Practical example: a women's football team's week

The social media manager of a women's football team plays on Sundays. Her Weekly base has four slots: Monday result, Wednesday training clip, Friday pre-match and Sunday line-up. On Monday morning she opens the library, clicks Fill my week to get inspiration for the empty days, swaps two suggestions for posts from her saved board "Matchday reels", and writes the captions in each entry. On Friday she moves the pre-match reel to In production, and on Sunday night everything is Published. She sends the PDF of the week to the coach on Monday.

Limits

  • The Content Plan is part of Plus and Pro (and Enterprise). Starter doesn't include it.
  • Plans are private to each user; there is no shared team calendar. To share, export the PDF, Excel or .ics file.
  • SCP doesn't publish to your social accounts; the plan is where you organise, not where you post.

As a sports content calendar linked to real posts, it turns Sports Content Playbook into one of the sports social media tools you use every week. Next: how to build a monthly content plan for your club.

Frequently asked questions

Which plans include the Content Plan?

Plus and Pro, and Enterprise (My Club), which includes everything in Pro. Starter doesn't include it.

Can my teammates see my Content Plan?

No. Plans are private to each user. Share the week or month by downloading it as PDF, Excel or a calendar file.

Can I import the plan into Google Calendar?

Yes. Download it as Calendar (.ics) and open the file in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

Does SCP publish the posts for me?

No. SCP doesn't connect to your social accounts. The Content Plan organises what you will publish.
