How to adapt a content idea to your club or organization

Content LibraryUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

To adapt a content idea in Sports Content Playbook, run the AI analysis on the post and use its How to adapt it to your club section and its Ready to use block (3 hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft with placeholders), then drag the post to your Content Plan, where the AI's adaptation goes into the notes.

Adapt, don't copy

A post that worked for another club worked for its audience, with its players and its moment. Your version has to keep what made it work (the hook, the structure, the pacing) and change everything else: your players, your venue, your sponsor, your language. Sports Content Playbook is built for exactly that step, so the social media ideas for sports teams you find become posts that fit yours. It shows you real posts from clubs, leagues and brands, explains why they worked and gives you a starting script for your own. It works the same for a club, a league, a federation, an agency or a sports brand.

Note that you can't download the original video or image, and you shouldn't reuse the original caption as is. The goal is a new post made with your own assets.

Step 1: pick an idea you can actually make

  1. In the Content Library, open Filters.
  2. Under What works, select Replicable ideas: formats you can recreate with your own players, venue and footage.
  3. Under Inspiration from, choose your Sport and, if you like, a Competition or Club similar to yours. Clubs like mine uses the competitions and sports in your profile.
  4. Under Where & how, pick the Format and Platform you publish on.
  5. Open a post and use More like this to see other versions of the same idea (same format, sport and competition).

Step 2: let the AI write your starting version

  1. On the post, click Unlock AI Analysis (uses 1 analysis), then View AI Analysis.
  2. Read How to adapt it to your club. If it says Hard to replicate, the idea depends on something you probably don't have; go back and pick another.
  3. In Hook, click Copy on the Template and fill it with your club's details.
  4. In Ready to use, choose one of the 3 Hook ideas, follow the Shot list (3-6 shots, each with a tip) and edit the Caption draft, replacing every [placeholder].

See how to analyze a content idea for every section of the analysis.

Step 3: put it in your plan

On Plus and Pro, the Content Plan is your sports content planning tool. Drag the analysed post onto a day in the Content Plan panel of the library (or use the + button, which works on phones). The entry keeps the original post as inspiration, with its link, and the AI's adaptation goes into its Notes. Set the type (Post, Carousel, Reel, Story, YouTube, TikTok), write your Caption and move the status from Planned to In production and Published. On Starter, save the post to a board in Saved boards and keep the analysis in My AI analyses.

Example: from a big club's reel to a second-division club

A second-division football club looking for football social media content ideas finds a Replicable reel where a top-flight club's players guess their teammates' baby photos. The analysis says the hook is the first wrong guess and suggests using the club's own squad and training ground. The social media manager copies the hook template, uses the shot list on Thursday's training session and replaces the [placeholders] in the caption draft with the club's hashtag and the next match. For sports Instagram content ideas like this one, the format travels; only the faces and the details change.

Leagues, federations and brands work the same way: a federation can adapt a club's matchday format to its national team, and a brand can use the Brands & sponsors filters (Pro) to see how sponsors are integrated before adapting the idea to its own partnership.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a post only because it has huge views. Check How it performed: it compares the post with its own account.
  • Keeping the original caption or graphics. Use the caption draft and your own assets.
  • Skipping the hook. Most of what makes a post replicable is in the first seconds; that's why the analysis gives you a template and three alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download the original post's video to edit it?

No. SCP doesn't let you download posts' videos or images, and bulk copying is against the terms. You recreate the idea with your own footage.

What if the analysis says Hard to replicate?

It means the idea depends on something most clubs don't have, such as a star player or a big production. Use More like this or the Replicable ideas filter to find a version you can make.

Does the AI adaptation appear in my Content Plan?

Yes. When you drag an analysed post to the Content Plan (Plus and Pro), the AI's adaptation is added to the entry's notes.

Does this work for leagues, federations and brands?

Yes. The steps are the same; the analysis' adaptation is written for a club, so read it with your organization's audience in mind.
