How to use the Content Library

Content LibraryRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

The Content Library in Sports Content Playbook lets you browse 500,000+ real posts from clubs, leagues and brands: filter them in the left column, open a post to watch it and read its caption and tags, save it to a private board, run an AI analysis or share a public link to that single post.

How the library is laid out

The Content Library (content-library.html) is where you land after logging in. It is the main source of sports social media content ideas in Sports Content Playbook, with sports content ideas from every competition, and is included in every plan.

  • Left column: Filters, with Clear all and Hide filters. Below them, My space: My AI analyses, Content Plan and Saved boards.
  • Top of the feed: the search box, the period (This week, This month, This season, All time, Custom dates…) and the sort (Latest, Most viewed, Surprise me).
  • The feed: post cards. Each shows the network, format, length, views and likes.

What you can do with each post

Every card has four buttons:

  • Drag to your Content Plan (Plus and Pro).
  • Save to one or more private boards.
  • AI analysis: unlock an analysis of why the post worked and how to adapt it.
  • Public link: anyone can open that single post, no account needed, and the link never expires.

Click the card to open the post detail: the original post embedded and playable, Tags and caption (the caption with a Copy button, hashtags and tags; click a tag to filter the library by it), More like this (same format, sport and competition), Save and, once analysed, Download PDF of the analysis.

Step by step: from browsing to a saved board

  1. Open the library and pick a section in the left column, for example Where & how → Platform → Instagram.
  2. Add a second filter such as Inspiration from → Sport.
  3. Choose a period and sort by Most viewed.
  4. Open a card, play the post and read Tags and caption.
  5. Click Save, choose a board or create one with New board.
  6. Use More like this to find similar posts and save the best ones too.
  7. Find everything later in Saved boards (saved.html), where you can sort boards by Recent, A–Z or Most posts, rename or delete them and copy links.

Example: a basketball club planning Instagram and TikTok

A basketball club's content team wants sports Instagram content ideas for a derby week and sports TikTok content ideas for their younger audience. They filter Platform by Instagram, Sport by basketball and Match Day → Pre-match, save six posts to a "Derby" board, then switch the platform to TikTok and repeat in a second board. The team lead copies the public link of the best TikTok and sends it to the player who will star in their version.

Good to know

  • Metrics refresh when a post is played, at most every 6 hours.
  • No downloads: you cannot download a post's video or image, and bulk copying is against the terms.
  • Missing account? Ask SCP to add it via contact; it is included in every plan.
  • Some filters depend on your plan: Content Creators from Plus, Brands & sponsors in Pro; Match Day is in every plan.

Without a plan, the library appears blurred with the plans and a one-click 7-day trial.

Frequently asked questions

Are my saved boards visible to others?

No. Saved boards are private. To share a post, use its Public link; to share a search, copy the link from the address bar.

Can I save the same post to several boards?

Yes. A post can be saved to one or more boards, and you can also drag posts onto a board from the side panel.

Why do the views on a post change?

Metrics refresh when the post is played, at most every 6 hours, so numbers keep updating as the post grows.

Which networks are in the library?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Facebook, from 770+ clubs and 110+ competitions in 17 sports.
