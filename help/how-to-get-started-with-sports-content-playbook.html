How to get started with Sports Content Playbook

Getting StartedRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

To get started with Sports Content Playbook, sign up at signup.html with your email or Google, start the 7-day free trial, follow the 5-step guided tour in the Content Library and fill in your favourites in Profile so the library shows posts relevant to your club first.

1. Create your account

Sports Content Playbook is one of the sports social media tools you can start using in a couple of minutes.

  1. Go to signup.html.
  2. Enter your First name, Last name and Email, then click Continue. You will receive an email link to finish signing up. Or use Continue with Google.
  3. If you came from a plan card on the pricing page, you will see the Selected plan and the 7-day free trial.

Next time, log in with your email and password or with Google. If you signed up with the email link, set a password in Profile → Account → Password (minimum 8 characters). Forgot it? Use Forgot your password? Set a new one on the login page to get a reset email.

2. Start your free trial

After logging in you land in the Content Library. Without a plan, the library appears blurred with the plans on top and a one-click 7-day trial. The trial needs a card, charges €0 today and includes 5 AI analyses. Cancel during the trial and nothing is charged; you get a reminder email before it ends. One trial per person or organisation.

Not sure which plan? See pricing: Starter covers the library, Plus adds the Content Creators filter and the Content Plan, and Pro adds the Brands & sponsors filters.

3. Follow the guided tour

The first time you open the library, a 5-step tour shows you around:

  1. Filters, in the left column.
  2. Content Creators.
  3. The main filters: Inspiration from, Match Day and Brands & sponsors.
  4. My space: My AI analyses, Content Plan and Saved boards.
  5. The buttons on each post.

You can replay it at any time with the ? next to My space.

4. Personalise your profile

Open Profile and fill in your sport, channels and Favourites: your favourite network, up to 5 favourite profiles (clubs, leagues or brands) and up to 5 favourite creators. These power three features:

  • Favs: posts from your favourite profiles.
  • Clubs like mine: posts from the competitions and sports in your profile.
  • Fill my week: fills empty days of your Content Plan with posts from your favourites (Plus and Pro).

You can switch between English and Spanish from the menu or in Profile → Account → Website language, which also sets the language of your emails.

Example: a basketball academy's first session

The communications lead of a basketball academy signs up with Google, starts the trial and follows the tour. In Profile she picks Instagram as favourite network and adds three clubs from her league and two youth-focused clubs she admires. Back in the library, Favs now shows their latest posts, and Clubs like mine gives her social media ideas for sports teams in her own competition. Ten minutes in, she has saved four sports content ideas to a board called "November".

Common first-day mistakes

  • Skipping Favourites. Without them, Favs, Clubs like mine and Fill my week have nothing to work with.
  • Spending AI analyses too fast. The trial includes 5. Filter first, then analyse only the posts you would really adapt.
  • Looking for a missing club and giving up. Ask SCP to add it via contact; it is included in every plan.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sign up without a password?

Yes. Use the email link or Continue with Google. You can add a password later in Profile → Account → Password.

Why is the library blurred?

Your account has no active plan yet. Start the 7-day free trial or choose a plan to unlock it.

Can I change my email address?

Not by yourself. Write to info@sportscontentplaybook.com if you need help with your account.

How do I see the tour again?

Click the ? next to My space in the left column of the Content Library.
