How does AI work in Sports Content Playbook?

AI ToolsRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Sports Content Playbook uses AI in two places: smart filters, AI labels that SCP applies to the posts in the library so you can filter by moment, angle or sponsor, and the AI analysis, which you unlock on a single post to get its hook, why it worked, how it performed and a ready-to-use version for your club. SCP has no AI chat or free-text generator.

Two kinds of AI in SCP

Most AI tools for sports content creation start from a blank prompt. Sports Content Playbook starts from real posts: more than 500,000 from clubs, leagues and brands. The AI works on top of that library in two ways:

  • Smart filters (automatic, for everyone): SCP's AI tags every post with labels such as format, moment or angle, so you can filter the library. You don't spend anything to use them.
  • AI analysis (on demand): you choose a post and the AI explains it in depth and writes a starting version for your club. Each one uses 1 analysis from your monthly allowance.

SCP only uses public posts. You don't connect your social accounts, and SCP doesn't need access to them.

Smart filters: AI labels on every post

The filters in the Content Library are filled in by AI. Two sections depend on it most:

  • Match Day (every plan): Pre-match / Live / Post-match timeline, Result, Opponent, Transfers, Kit, Season moment, Campaign moment, Angle and tone, and more.
  • Brands & sponsors (Pro): Business goal, Sponsors, Integration, Sponsor sector, and more.

These labels are detected by AI on Instagram posts from October 2026; older posts are being analysed. The search box also helps: type a sentence such as transfer reels with a sponsor that worked and it turns your words into filters. That sentence search is free; it doesn't use AI analyses.

AI analysis: how it reads a post

  • Videos (reels, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook): the AI watches frames of the whole video and reads an automatic transcript of what is said. It takes about 3 minutes. If the video can't be read, it falls back to the cover.
  • Other posts (images, carousels): up to a minute.
  • Numbers: the How it performed verdict isn't the AI's opinion. It is computed from the post's real numbers against the median of its account that quarter.
  • Language: analyses are written in English and translated to Spanish the first time someone opens them in Spanish.
  • Limits by design: the AI never invents numbers and never identifies people by their face.

What you get: Summary, Hook (with a reusable template), Why it works, How it performed, Format, Angle, Visual breakdown or Slide by slide, Scroll stoppers, How to adapt it to your club, and a Ready to use block with 3 hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft. Your AI allowance

  1. Each user gets AI analyses every month: Starter 5, Plus 10, Pro 30 (5 during the free trial).
  2. They renew monthly from your subscription date; unused ones don't carry over.
  3. See what's left in My space → My AI analyses, or in Profile → Billing & membership.
  4. Need more? Buy more AI analyses in Billing & membership or on the pricing page. Packs never expire and are used after the monthly ones.
  5. On team plans, analyses unlocked by teammates are free for you to read. Each user still has their own allowance.

Example: a handball federation

The communications team of a handball federation uses the smart filters every week without spending anything: Match Day → Post-match, Sport: handball, period This month. When a post stands out, one person runs the AI analysis. On their team plan, the rest of the team reads it for free in My AI analyses → My team's, so the federation's 30 monthly analyses per Pro user go a long way.

What the AI does not do

Compared with other sports social media tools, sports marketing tools and sports content creation tools, it helps to be clear about what SCP's AI is not:

  • There is no AI chat and no free-text caption, script or idea generator.
  • It doesn't publish or schedule posts on your networks.
  • It doesn't read your private accounts or analytics.

The AI's writing help is inside each analysis: the hook template, hook ideas, shot list and caption draft. See how to generate content ideas using AI for a workflow that combines everything.

Frequently asked questions

Do smart filters use my AI analyses?

No. Smart filters and sentence search are free. Only unlocking an AI analysis on a post uses 1 analysis.

Does SCP need access to my social media accounts?

No. SCP works only with public posts, and you don't connect your accounts.

Can the AI be wrong?

The labels and analyses are generated by AI, so read them with judgement. The How it performed verdict is computed from real numbers, and the AI never invents figures.

What happens if an analysis fails?

If generation fails, the analysis isn't counted against your allowance.
