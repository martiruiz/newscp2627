How to set up your club in My Club

My ClubRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

My Club isn't self-serve: the Sports Content Playbook team sets it up with you. You send the club's official accounts, players, sponsors, sections and the emails of your team; SCP links the accounts, creates the sections, adds the members and uploads your club's posts every day into a private space.

What My Club is

My Club is the Enterprise space of Sports Content Playbook: a private library with the posts of your own club, its players, sponsors, teams and sections (first team, women's team, academy, other sports), plus your competitors and the content creators linked to you. It has the same filters, saving and AI analysis as the Content Library, and it includes everything in Pro.

A space can also be a competition, an agency or a federation; it then shows as "My competition", "My agency" or "My federation". Only your club's marketing and communications team can see it.

Step by step: getting your club's space set up

  1. Book a demo at book-a-call.html (30 minutes) or write to us from the contact page. Enterprise has a custom price, from €99/month, and a 7-day trial that SCP sets up for you.
  2. Prepare your list of accounts: the club's official profiles on each network, the accounts of each section, your players' accounts (and which team they play in), your sponsors' accounts, the competitors you want to follow and the content creators who work with the club.
  3. Send the emails of your team: the people in marketing and communications who should have access. Members are added by SCP.
  4. SCP links the accounts and creates the sections and teams. From then on SCP uploads your club's posts every day.
  5. Sign in with the email SCP added and open My Club. You'll see the tabs Club profiles, Competitors, Players, Content creators and Sponsors, with the Section and Team filters.

If you open My Club and see "Your account isn't linked to a club yet. Write to us and we'll set up your club's space.", your email hasn't been linked yet. Write to SCP or use Book a demo.

What you see and do once it's ready

  • Club profiles: your official accounts' posts, filtered by section and team.
  • Players, Sponsors, Competitors and Content creators: tap one or more names to see only their posts.
  • Filter, save to Saved boards and unlock AI analyses exactly as in the library. On team plans, analyses unlocked by a teammate are free to read.
  • Drag posts to your Content Plan, which is included because My Club includes everything in Pro.

A tab only appears when it has linked accounts, and a name marked "Account linked, no posts imported yet" will fill up as SCP uploads its posts.

Practical example: a multi-sport club

A club with a football first team, a women's team, an academy and a basketball section sends SCP one list: the club's Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, the basketball section's own Instagram, 40 player accounts grouped by team, six sponsors and three rival clubs. SCP creates the sections Football and Basketball with their teams and adds the four people in the comms department. On Monday the social media manager opens My Club, picks Section Football and Team Women's team, and sees in one place what the players and sponsors posted over the weekend. That's where the social media ideas for sports teams that fit this club come from.

Limits worth knowing

  • Members can't add accounts, players, teams or sponsors themselves. To add or change anything, ask SCP through the contact page or your account manager.
  • Posts are uploaded daily, not in real time.
  • The AI inside My Club is the same AI analysis as in the library; there is no separate AI tool for your club.

Used together with the library, My Club makes Sports Content Playbook one of the sports social media tools and sports marketing tools that shows your own club next to the rest, which helps you build a sports social media strategy on real data. Next: how to manage players, teams and club sections.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set up My Club myself?

No. Setup is done with the SCP team: you send the accounts and the team's emails, and SCP links them, creates the sections and adds the members.

Which plan includes My Club?

Enterprise, with a custom price from €99/month that is explained in a 30-minute demo. It includes everything in Pro.

Who can see my club's space?

Only the members SCP adds, normally your club's marketing and communications team. The space is private.

Can a league, agency or federation use My Club?

Yes. The space can be a club, a competition, an agency or a federation, and shows as My competition, My agency or My federation.
