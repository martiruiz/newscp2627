How to create captions, hooks and scripts with AI

AI ToolsUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Sports Content Playbook doesn't have a free-text caption or script generator. Every AI analysis ends with ready-to-use material instead — a reusable Hook template, 3 Hook ideas, a Shot list and a Caption draft with [placeholders] — that you turn into your own hook, short script and caption in a few minutes.

What the AI gives you (and what it doesn't)

Sports Content Playbook is one of the AI tools for sports content creation that starts from a real post, not from a blank prompt. When you analyse a post from the library, the AI breaks down why it worked and hands you four pieces of writing you can reuse:

  • Hook: the original hook, its category, why it works and a reusable Template with a Copy button.
  • Hook ideas: 3 alternative openings, inside Ready to use.
  • Shot list: 3 to 6 shots, each with a tip.
  • Caption draft: up to 3 lines with [placeholders] for your club's details, plus the post's topics as hashtags.

There is no AI chat, no idea generator and no box where you type a prompt to get a caption or script. The writing help in SCP is exactly these four pieces, always tied to a post you chose.

Step by step: from an analysis to your hook, script and caption

  1. Open the Content Library and find a post close to what you want to publish. The search box and the Replicable ideas filter are a good start.
  2. Open the post and click Unlock AI Analysis, then View AI Analysis. This uses 1 analysis from your monthly allowance; reopening it later is free. Videos take about 3 minutes because the AI watches frames of the whole video and reads a transcript.
  3. Hook: read why the original hook works, press Copy on the Template and fill the gaps with your own player, opponent or moment. If it doesn't fit, pick one of the 3 Hook ideas in Ready to use.
  4. Script: use the Shot list as the backbone. Write one line per shot: what we see, what is said or shown on screen. For pacing, check the seconds in Visual breakdown (or Slide by slide for a carousel).
  5. Caption: copy the Caption draft, replace every [placeholder] with real facts and add the topic hashtags you want.
  6. On Plus and Pro, drag the analysed post to your Content Plan: the AI's adaptation goes into the entry's notes, and you can paste your caption into the Caption field.

Practical example: a matchday reel for a second-division club

The social media manager of a second-division football club wants sports reels ideas for Friday's pre-match. She filters Match Day by Pre-match, finds an Overperforming reel where a player answers quick questions in the tunnel, and unlocks the analysis.

  • Hook: she copies the template and writes her own version with the captain's name and Sunday's opponent.
  • Script: the shot list has five shots. She turns them into a five-line script for the shoot: wide shot of the tunnel, close-up of the captain, three questions, the last answer, the club crest with the kick-off time.
  • Caption: she fills the [placeholders] of the caption draft with the opponent, the date and the ticket link, and keeps two hashtags.

Total time: about ten minutes, and every line is based on a format that already worked for a similar club.

Tips and limits

  • Rewrite in your club's voice. The drafts are a starting point; your fans know how your club sounds.
  • Fill every placeholder with real data. The AI never invents numbers, so scores, dates and names are yours to add and check.
  • Adapt per network. The caption draft is short on purpose. Expand it for sports Instagram content ideas, keep it shorter for TikTok.
  • Skip "Hard to replicate" posts if you need something fast; look for Replicable ideas instead.
  • The analysis is written in English and translated into Spanish the first time someone opens it in Spanish.

Frequently asked questions

Can Sports Content Playbook write a caption from scratch?

No. There is no free-text caption or script generator. The AI writes a Caption draft, Hook ideas and a Shot list for each post you analyse, and you adapt them.

Do the hook ideas cost extra analyses?

No. They are part of the AI analysis, which uses 1 analysis from your allowance the first time you open it. Reopening it later is free.

Can I save or share the hooks and caption draft?

Yes. You can download the analysis as a PDF, and on team plans your teammates can read the analyses you unlocked without using their own allowance.

Can I get the drafts in Spanish?

Yes. Switch the website to Spanish and the analysis is translated the first time it is opened in Spanish.
