How does the 7-day free trial work?

Account & BillingRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Every Sports Content Playbook plan starts with a 7-day free trial of the plan you choose. You add a card but pay €0 today, you get 5 AI analyses, and if you cancel before the trial ends nothing is charged. There is one trial per person or organisation, and you receive a reminder email before it ends.

How the trial works

The free trial lets you use Sports Content Playbook for 7 days before paying. You pick a plan (Starter, Plus or Pro), and during the trial you have that plan's features: the Content Library with its filters, AI analysis and, on Plus and Pro, the Content Plan. The trial includes 5 AI analyses, whatever plan you chose.

  • A card is required to start, but the charge today is €0.
  • Cancel during the trial and nothing is charged.
  • SCP sends you a reminder email before the trial ends.
  • There is one trial per person and per organisation.

Start your trial step by step

  1. Open pricing.html, choose Monthly or Yearly and click Start 7-day free trial on the plan you want. If you're already logged in without a plan, the Content Library shows the plans and lets you start the trial in one click.
  2. If you don't have an account yet, sign up with First name, Last name and Email, or with Continue with Google. The page shows your Selected plan and the 7-day free trial.
  3. Before you pay, a summary shows Today (€0), Trial ends, First charge and Then. If you have a promo code, enter it in Discount code.
  4. Click Start free trial and add your card.
  5. You land in the Content Library. A guided tour shows you the filters, My space and the buttons on each post the first time.

What happens when the trial ends

If you don't cancel, your subscription starts automatically on the plan you chose:

  • Monthly plans charge on the 1st of each month (Madrid time). Your first charge is prorated: it covers only the days from the end of the trial until the next 1st, and from then on you pay the full monthly price every 1st.
  • Yearly plans charge the yearly price when the trial ends and renew on that anniversary.

From the first paid period, your AI analyses follow your plan: 5 a month on Starter, 10 on Plus and 30 on Pro.

Cancel or change plan during the trial

  1. Go to Profile → Billing & membership.
  2. To stop, click Cancel membership. You keep access until the trial ends and nothing is charged. If you change your mind, click Keep my membership.
  3. To try another plan, click Change plan. During the trial the change applies at once and nothing is charged.

Your Saved boards and plans are kept if you cancel, so you can pick up where you left off if you subscribe later.

Example: getting the most from 7 days

The social media manager of a basketball club starts a Plus trial on a Monday. Day 1, she fills in her Profile favourites (5 clubs, 5 creators, favourite network) so Favs and Clubs like mine show relevant sports content ideas. Day 2, she uses Match Day filters to find post-match posts from her competition and saves the best to a board. Days 3 to 5, she spends her 5 AI analyses on the posts she would actually copy and reads each Hook template and Shot list. Day 6, she drags them into the Content Plan and uses Fill my week. Before day 7 she decides whether Plus is the right fit among her sports social media tools, or switches plan.

Spend the trial analyses carefully: they are the part of the AI tools for sports content creation that is limited, while browsing and filtering the library is unlimited.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a card for a free trial?

The card lets the subscription continue without interruption when the trial ends. You pay €0 today and nothing is charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

How many AI analyses do I get in the trial?

5 AI analyses during the 7-day trial, on any plan. Opening an analysis you already unlocked again is free.

Can I have a second trial?

No. There is one free trial per person and per organisation.

Is there a trial for My Club?

Yes, a 7-day trial is available for Enterprise (My Club), but it is set up by the SCP team. Book a demo at book-a-call.html to arrange it.
