What are Top Picks, Replicable Ideas and Overperforming?

Content LibraryUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Top Picks, Replicable ideas and Overperforming are three special selections in the Sports Content Playbook Content Library: Top Picks are the best posts chosen by the SCP team, Replicable ideas are formats you can recreate with your own assets, and Overperforming are posts that got at least 2x the usual results of their own account.

Three shortcuts to ideas worth copying

The Content Library of Sports Content Playbook holds more than 500,000 posts from 770+ clubs and 110+ competitions. Filters by sport, competition or format narrow that down, but they don't tell you which posts are worth your time. These three selections do: each one answers a different question a club's social media team asks before planning the week.

  • Top Picks: which posts are simply the best? (an editorial choice)
  • Replicable ideas: which posts can I copy with what I already have? (an effort filter)
  • Overperforming: which posts did better than expected for their own account? (a numbers filter)

You can combine them with any other filter, so they work as a quick way to find sports content ideas and social media ideas for sports teams that fit your club.

Top Picks: the best posts, picked by SCP

Top Picks is an editorial selection made by the SCP team: posts we consider excellent examples of sports social media, whatever the club's size. It is not a button in the filter column. To open it, type top picks in the search box (Search: signings, a club, reels…) and press Enter. You can then add filters on top, for example Sport or Platform.

Replicable ideas: easy to copy with your own assets

Replicable ideas sits in the What works section of the filters. It marks formats you can recreate with your own players, venue and footage, without a big production: no celebrities, no expensive shoots. Every post in Challenges (players doing a test, game or contest) is also Replicable. This is the selection to start from when your team is small or you need something for tomorrow.

Overperforming: posts that beat their own account

Overperforming, also in What works, compares each post with its own account, not with the whole library. That way a small club's post that did very well for that club shows up next to the giants. The rules:

  • The post got at least 2x the median of its account's posts in the same quarter.
  • It uses views, or likes + comments when a post has no views.
  • Only accounts with 8 or more posts that quarter are counted, so the median is meaningful.
  • Posts with lots of views but very few likes are left out.
  • The list is recomputed every hour.

How to use them, step by step

  1. Open the Content Library.
  2. In Filters, under What works, click Replicable ideas or Overperforming. For Top Picks, type top picks in the search box.
  3. Add your context from Inspiration from: Sport, Competition or Club.
  4. Choose a period, for example This month, and sort by Latest or Most viewed.
  5. Open a post, then Save it to a board, run an AI analysis or drag it to your Content Plan (Plus and Pro).
  6. If you'll come back to this combination, save it from Saved searches….

Example: a second-division football club

The social media manager of a second-division football club works alone and needs sports reels ideas for a quiet week. She selects Replicable ideas + Overperforming, Sport: football, Format: reels, period This month. The result is a short list of reels that clubs made with their own squad and that beat their account's usual numbers. She saves three to a board called "Training week" and analyses the one she likes most. That is a sports social media strategy built on evidence rather than guesswork.

Overperforming says a post did well for its account. It doesn't guarantee the same result for yours: adapt the idea to your audience. See how to adapt a content idea to your club.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine Replicable ideas and Overperforming?

Yes. Select both in What works and you get posts that are easy to copy and also beat their account's usual results. You can add any other filter on top.

Why is a post from a small club in Overperforming and not a viral post from a big club?

Overperforming compares each post with the median of its own account that quarter. A big club's post with millions of views can be normal for that account, while a small club's post can be 2x its usual.

Where is the Top Picks button?

There isn't one in the filter column. Type top picks in the library search box to open the selection.

How often is Overperforming updated?

Every hour. Post metrics refresh when a post is played, at most every 6 hours, so very recent posts can enter the list later.
