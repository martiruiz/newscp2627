What is the difference between Starter, Plus and Pro?

Account & BillingUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Starter (€9/month) gives you the Content Library, search, smart filters, Match Day, Saved boards and 5 AI analyses a month. Plus (€19/month) adds the Content Creators filter, the Content Plan and 10 AI analyses. Pro (€49/month) adds the Brands & sponsors filters and 30 AI analyses. Prices are per user, VAT included, and Enterprise (My Club) is a custom plan from €99/month.

The three plans in one sentence each

Sports Content Playbook sits among sports social media tools and sports marketing tools as a research and planning platform. All its plans include the Content Library of real posts from clubs, leagues and brands, and AI analysis, so even Starter gives you AI tools for sports content creation (Hook template, Hook ideas, Shot list and Caption draft inside each analysis). The difference is how many extra tools and how many AI analyses you get:

  • Starter: the library and its filters, to find sports content ideas every week.
  • Plus: Starter plus the Content Creators filter and the Content Plan, a sports content planning tool with weekly and monthly calendars.
  • Pro: Plus plus the Brands & sponsors filters, for teams that sell and activate sponsorships.

Prices are in euros, VAT included, per user. The yearly price works out at about two months free. The Starter and Plus launch prices are available to people who join until 31 December 2026, and you keep them for good. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

How to choose your plan

  1. Pick Starter if you mainly need inspiration: browsing what works, filtering by sport, competition and Match Day moment, and saving posts to boards.
  2. Pick Plus if you also plan the week: drag posts from the library onto a day, use Fill my week, set a Weekly base and export the week to PDF, Excel or .ics. Plus also unlocks content creators, useful if you work with influencers or look at creator formats.
  3. Pick Pro if sponsors matter: the Brands & sponsors filters (Business goal, Sponsors, Integration, Sponsor sector) show how other clubs integrate partners, and 30 AI analyses a month cover a busy team.
  4. Talk to SCP about Enterprise if you want My Club: your club's own space with the posts of your players, sponsors, teams and sections, set up with the SCP team. It includes everything in Pro and is explained in a 30-minute demo.

To compare live prices and start a trial, open pricing.html.

Example: which plan fits which team

A basketball academy with one person doing social media wants weekly ideas for training videos and player features. Starter is enough: the library, Match Day and 5 AI analyses a month.

A second-division football club with a manager and a video editor plans every week and wants to see how creators film matchday content. Plus for two users gives each of them the Content Plan and 10 AI analyses.

A handball federation that must deliver sponsor posts every month chooses Pro, so the team can filter by Sponsor sector and Integration and has 30 AI analyses per user.

Good to know

  • Prices are per user. You can add up to 5 users from the billing page; for more, request a custom plan.
  • AI analyses renew monthly from your subscription date and unused ones don't carry over. If you run out, you can buy one-off packs that never expire.
  • You can upgrade at any time and it applies straight away, prorated. A downgrade applies at the end of your current period.
  • Every plan works only with public posts: you don't connect your own social media accounts.

Not sure? Start the 7-day trial on Plus or Pro and change plan during the trial: it applies at once and nothing is charged.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Content Plan included in Starter?

No. The Content Plan is included in Plus and Pro (and Enterprise). Starter includes the Content Library, search, smart filters, Match Day, Saved boards and 5 AI analyses a month.

Which plan includes the Brands & sponsors filters?

Pro and Enterprise. Match Day filters are available on every plan and the Content Creators filter starts with Plus.

Are the prices per user or per club?

Per user, in euros with VAT included. A club with three people on Plus pays three times the Plus price.

What does Enterprise add over Pro?

My Club: a private space with your own club's posts (players, sponsors, teams and sections), set up with the SCP team. It has a custom price from €99/month.
