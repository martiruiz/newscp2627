One idea, many posts

How to turn one content idea into multiple social media posts

Tips & Best PracticesUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

In Sports Content Playbook you turn one idea into several posts by hand, with three real features: search the same topic in other formats and platforms with the Format and Platform filters, use the AI analysis (Hook template, Hook ideas, Shot list, Caption draft) as the brief for each version, and plan each version as its own Reel, TikTok, Carousel or Story entry in the Content Plan. SCP does not convert or resize content automatically.

How it works

One good idea (a signing, a derby, a kit launch, a player milestone) can feed a whole week if you tell it in the format each network rewards. Sports Content Playbook helps you see how other clubs told the same kind of story as a reel, a TikTok or a carousel, explains why those posts worked, and lets you plan every version side by side.

What SCP does not do: it doesn't edit, convert or resize your content, it doesn't write captions from scratch, and you can't download other clubs' videos or images. The writing help is inside each AI analysis: a reusable Hook template, 3 Hook ideas, a Shot list and a Caption draft.

Step by step: from one idea to four posts

  1. Pick the core idea. In the Content Library, type the topic in the search box, for example “transfers”, or use the Transfers filter in Match Day. Add Overperforming to keep the posts that beat their account's usual.
  2. See the idea in each format. In Where & how, change Format and Platform to compare reels, carousels and TikTok posts on the same topic. You can also type a sentence such as “transfer carousels”. On a post you like, More like this shows more posts of the same format, sport and competition.
  3. Analyse one reference per format. Click Unlock AI Analysis. For a video you get a Visual breakdown with seconds; for a carousel, Slide by slide. Under Ready to use you get 3 Hook ideas, a Shot list and a Caption draft with [placeholders].
  4. Plan each version. Drag the analysed posts into the Content Plan panel (or use + to pick a day on your phone). Each entry keeps the inspiration post and the AI's adaptation in its notes. Open each entry and set the type: Reel, TikTok, Carousel or Story.
  5. Write each caption. Start from the Caption draft and the Hook ideas, and adapt them per network in the entry's Caption field. Move each entry from Planned to In production and Published as you go.

Example: a second-division football club signs a striker

The club announces a new striker on Wednesday. The social media manager searches “transfers” with Overperforming on and finds three references from her league.

  • Reel (Instagram): an announcement reel. The Shot list gives her 5 shots to film at the stadium: arrival, shirt reveal, first touch on the pitch. This is where most sports reels ideas start, and Instagram is where the club's audience is largest.
  • TikTok: filtering Platform to TikTok, she sees that transfer TikToks favour a lighter tone, such as a teammate quiz. She uses one of the 3 Hook ideas as the opening line. Sports TikTok content ideas rarely work as a copy of the reel, so she films a different take the same day.
  • Carousel: the Slide by slide of a reference carousel shows a structure she copies with her own assets: photo, previous clubs, a quote, the shirt number.
  • Stories: SCP doesn't produce stories, so she plans two Story entries in the Content Plan, a teaser before the announcement and a share of the reel after, built from the same footage.

One shoot gives her four pieces in one week of the Content Plan, and the reel and carousel structures become sports Instagram content ideas she can reuse for the next signing.

Tips and limits

  • Plan the shoot once. Merge the Shot lists of your references before filming, so one session covers the reel, the TikTok and the carousel photos.
  • Change the hook, not just the size. Each network rewards a different opening; the 3 Hook ideas in the analysis give you alternatives to test.
  • Use Replicable ideas when your resources are limited: those formats can be recreated with your own players, venue and footage.
  • The Content Plan is on Plus and Pro. On Starter, save the references to a board per idea and keep your versions in your own document.
  • AI analyses count against your monthly allowance; reopening one you already unlocked is free.

Frequently asked questions

Can SCP turn my reel into a carousel automatically?

No. Sports Content Playbook doesn't edit or convert content. It shows you how others told the same idea in each format and gives you a Shot list, hooks and a Caption draft to produce each version yourself.

Can I download the reference videos to reuse them?

No. You can watch the embedded original post and share its public link, but you cannot download other accounts' videos or images.

How do I find the same idea on TikTok?

Search the topic in the Content Library and set the Platform filter to TikTok. You can combine it with Overperforming or your sport and competition.

Does the Caption draft work for every network?

It is a short draft of up to 3 lines with placeholders, based on the analysed post. Use it as a starting point and adapt the tone and length for each network.
