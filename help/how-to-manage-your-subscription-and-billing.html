How to manage your subscription and billing

Account & BillingUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

You manage your Sports Content Playbook subscription in Profile → Billing & membership. There you can change plan, switch to yearly, update your card and download invoices, add up to 5 users, buy more AI analyses and cancel your membership. Upgrades apply at once (prorated); downgrades and cancellations apply at the end of the period you've paid for.

Where to find your billing page

Open Profile and choose Billing & membership. It shows your plan, price, number of users, next charge, card and any discount, plus how many AI analyses you have left (“X of Y AI analyses left this month · Resets on …”). Below you'll find the table of invoices with a PDF for each one.

The buttons are Change plan, Switch to yearly, Billing details & invoices, Buy more AI analyses and Cancel membership.

Common tasks step by step

Change plan

  1. Click Change plan and pick Starter, Plus or Pro.
  2. An upgrade applies now and you pay the prorated difference. A downgrade applies at the end of your current period, and you keep your current plan until then.
  3. A scheduled change can be undone from the same page before it takes effect.

Switch to yearly

  1. Click Switch to yearly. Moving from monthly to yearly applies now, prorated. Yearly costs about two months less than paying monthly for 12 months.
  2. Going back from yearly to monthly applies at the end of the yearly period.

Update your card, billing details or invoices

  1. Click Billing details & invoices. It opens the secure Stripe portal, where you can change your card, edit the billing details that appear on your invoices and download past invoices.

Add or remove users

  1. In Your team, use the Users − / + buttons to set how many users your plan includes. The page shows what the change costs before you confirm. You can have up to 5 users; for more, click Request a custom plan.
  2. With a free seat, type the address in Colleague's email and click Add user. They get the same plan, with their own account and AI allowance, when they sign in with that email.
  3. To take someone off, click Remove next to their email.

Buy more AI analyses

  1. Click Buy more AI analyses (also available on pricing.html). Packs are one-off, never expire and are used after your monthly allowance.

Cancel your membership

  1. Click Cancel membership. You can leave an optional reason.
  2. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for (or the end of the trial). There are no further charges.
  3. About 3 days before your access ends, you receive an email. Until then you can click Keep my membership to undo it.

Your Saved boards and Content Plans are kept, so they're waiting for you if you come back.

Charges, payments and refunds

  • Prices are in euros, VAT included, per user. Euros are the only currency.
  • Monthly plans charge on the 1st of each month (Madrid time); the first charge is prorated up to the 1st. Yearly plans renew on their anniversary.
  • If a payment fails, you'll see “We couldn't take your last payment”. Click Update payment method to add a working card.
  • Promo codes are entered at checkout in Discount code; codes don't stack unless stated.
  • Payments are non-refundable and partial periods aren't refunded. In the EU and UK you have a 14-day right of withdrawal (refund minus the part used) by writing to info@sportscontentplaybook.com. See the terms.

Example: a club that grows its team

A handball club starts on Starter with one person. After a month, the club wants the Content Plan, so she clicks Change plan and picks Plus: it applies straight away and the difference is prorated. In January the club hires a video editor, so she adds him in Your team. Happy with the setup, she uses Switch to yearly to save about two months, and downloads each invoice in PDF for the club's accountant.

Comparing sports social media tools or sports marketing tools for your budget? The billing page shows every cost in one place, so you always know what you pay for your sports content creation tools.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the email of my account?

Not by yourself. Write to info@sportscontentplaybook.com and the SCP team will help you.

How do I delete my account?

Write to info@sportscontentplaybook.com asking for deletion. Cancelling your membership alone stops the charges but keeps your account.

If I downgrade, do I lose access immediately?

No. A downgrade applies at the end of your current period, and you keep your current plan's features until then.

Where do I get help with a billing problem?

Use contact.html?topic=support or write to info@sportscontentplaybook.com.
