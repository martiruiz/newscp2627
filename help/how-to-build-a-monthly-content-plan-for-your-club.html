How to build a monthly content plan for your club

Content PlannerUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

To build a monthly content plan in Sports Content Playbook, open the Content Plan in Month view, mark your fixtures and the key dates of the month, set a Weekly base of recurring posts, fill the gaps with posts from the Content Library and download the month as PDF, Excel or a calendar file. The Content Plan is included in Plus and Pro.

How a monthly plan works in SCP

The Content Plan has a Month view for the big picture and a Week view (days × hours) for the detail. A monthly plan in Sports Content Playbook combines three layers: fixed moments (matches, key dates, sponsor commitments), recurring posts from your Weekly base, and one-off ideas taken from real posts in the library. That mix is what turns a sports content calendar into a plan you can actually deliver.

Step by step: your month in 30 minutes

  1. Open Content Plan and choose Month. If the month has its own campaign, create it with New plan.
  2. Mark the fixed moments. Add an entry for each match day and sponsor commitment. Check the key dates the calendar shows for that month (for example Halloween or Black Friday); each opens the library filtered to that topic.
  3. Set your Weekly base with the posts you publish every week. In Week view they appear as red placeholders until you plan them, so no week starts empty.
  4. Find ideas in the library. In the Content Library, filter by your sport and competition, use Match Day for the pre-match, live and post-match moments, and save what you like to a board.
  5. Drag them into the month. From the library's Content Plan panel, drag posts onto a day or use the + to open a mini calendar. In the planner, Ideas & inspiration lists your saved boards to drag from.
  6. Park undecided ideas in the No date tray. Giving one a date later makes it Planned.
  7. Download the month. In Month view, Download exports the month on screen as PDF, Excel or Calendar (.ics), with the option to include "No date" ideas.

Practical example: November for a basketball club

A basketball club in the second tier has four league games in November and a sponsor activation with a local bank. The communications manager:

  • Creates a plan called "November" and adds the four game days and the bank activation.
  • Keeps a Weekly base of four posts: Monday result, Tuesday highlights reel, Thursday training clip, Saturday game-day graphic.
  • Opens the Black Friday key date to see what other clubs did, saves two merch posts and drags them onto that week.
  • Uses Replicable ideas to find two player challenges for the quieter week without games.
  • Downloads the month as Excel to share with the coach and the bank's marketing contact, and imports the .ics into the club's Google Calendar.

Every slot is tied to a real post that already worked, which gives the team social media ideas for sports teams it can trust.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Plan the structure, not every caption. Fill captions in the Week view a few days ahead, when you know results and line-ups.
  • Don't fill every day. Leave room for news (signings, injuries, results) that you can't plan.
  • Check the week every Monday. In the library, Fill my week fills empty days with posts from your favourite profiles, and shows "n/7 days with content".
  • Use the free calendar too. The Sports Content Calendar 2026-27 PDF in Free resources lists 28 dates for 9 sports to plan the season ahead.
  • Plans are private to each user. To share the month with your team, export it.

A monthly routine like this is the base of a sports social media strategy, and the Content Plan is the sports content planning tool that keeps it in one place. Next: how to organize your content workflow and team.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export a whole month?

Yes. Switch to Month view and use Download: the PDF, Excel or .ics file covers the month on screen. In Week view it covers that week.

Can I have different monthly plans for the first team and the women's team?

Yes. Use New plan to create separate plans, for example one per team or campaign, and switch between them.

Which key dates does the calendar include?

Twelve: New Year, Valentine's Day, Women's Day, April Fools, Earth Day, Pride, Pre-season, Fixture release, Halloween, Black Friday, Christmas and Inocentes.

Do I need Plus to build a monthly plan?

Yes. The Content Plan is included in Plus and Pro (and Enterprise). With Starter you can save posts to boards, but you don't have the calendar.
