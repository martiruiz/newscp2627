How to analyze a content idea

Content LibraryRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

To analyze a content idea in Sports Content Playbook, open the post in the Content Library and click Unlock AI Analysis: the AI breaks down its hook, format and angle, tells you how it performed against its own account and gives you hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft. Each analysis uses 1 of your monthly AI analyses; reopening it later is free.

What the AI analysis is

Finding sports content ideas is easy; knowing why a post worked and how to make your own version is the hard part. The AI analysis of Sports Content Playbook does that work for one post at a time. It is one of the AI tools for sports content creation included in every plan, and it complements the other sports content creation tools you already use, and it is written for the social media team of a club, league, federation or brand.

For videos (reels, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) the AI watches frames of the whole video and reads an automatic transcript of what is said. That takes about 3 minutes. Other posts take up to a minute. If a video can't be read, the analysis falls back to the cover image.

How to run an analysis, step by step

  1. Open the Content Library and find a post, for example with Replicable ideas or Overperforming.
  2. Click the post to open it, or use the AI analysis button on its card.
  3. Click Unlock AI Analysis. This uses 1 analysis from your monthly allowance. If generation fails, it isn't counted.
  4. When it's ready, click View AI Analysis.
  5. Use Download PDF if you want to share it with someone outside SCP.
  6. Find it again any time in My space → My AI analyses. Reopening it is free.

What each part of the analysis tells you

  • Summary: the post in two lines.
  • Hook: the opening text, its category, why it works and a reusable Template with a Copy button.
  • Why it works: the reasons behind the result.
  • How it performed: a verdict computed from the real numbers against the account's median that quarter: Above its usual (2x or more), In line with its usual, Below its usual (half or less), Too early to judge (counted less than 48 hours after publishing) or No numbers yet.
  • Format and Angle: category, type, layout, pacing, moment and style, plus the point of view.
  • Visual breakdown (videos, with seconds) or Slide by slide (carousels).
  • Scroll stoppers: what makes people stop.
  • How to adapt it to your club, or Hard to replicate when the idea depends on something you probably don't have.
  • Ready to use: 3 Hook ideas, a Shot list of 3-6 shots with a tip each, and a Caption draft of up to 3 lines with [placeholders]. Topics appear as hashtags.

Example: a basketball academy

A basketball academy wants sports reels ideas for its youth teams. Its coordinator filters by Sport: basketball, Format: reels and Challenges, and finds a shooting challenge from a professional club. The analysis shows the hook is a question in the first second, the verdict is Above its usual, and the Shot list is four simple shots on a single court. The academy copies the hook template, fills the [placeholders] with its own players' names and records it at the next training session.

Tips and limits

  • Spend analyses on posts you might really copy. Starter includes 5 per month, Plus 10 and Pro 30 (5 in the free trial). They renew monthly and unused ones don't carry over. You can buy one-off packs in Profile → Billing & membership → Buy more AI analyses.
  • On team plans, analyses unlocked by teammates are free for you to read ("Analysed by {name} from your team").
  • The AI never invents numbers and never identifies people by their face.
  • Analyses are written in English and translated to Spanish the first time someone opens them in Spanish.
  • You can't download the post's video or image; the analysis is the part you take away.

Frequently asked questions

Does opening an analysis again use another credit?

No. An analysis uses 1 of your monthly analyses the first time you unlock it; reopening it later is free.

How long does an analysis take?

About 3 minutes for videos, because the AI watches frames of the whole video and reads its transcript. Other posts take up to a minute.

What does Too early to judge mean?

The post's numbers were counted less than 48 hours after it was published, so it's too soon to compare it with its account's usual results.

Can I analyse posts that aren't in the library?

Analyses work on posts in the library. If an account you need is missing, ask SCP to add it through the contact page; it's included in every plan.
