How to manage players, teams and club sections

My ClubUpdated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

In My Club, players, teams and sections are managed by the Sports Content Playbook team on request: you send the changes (new signings, departures, a new team or section) and SCP updates them. You then use the Players tab and the Section and Team filters to see exactly the posts you need.

How players, teams and sections work

Your club's space in My Club is organised in sections (for example football, basketball or the academy) and, inside each section, teams (first team, women's team, U19…). Each player account is linked to the team it plays in. Accounts that aren't tied to one section, such as the club's main profile, show in every section.

Members can't add, move or remove players, teams or sections themselves. The SCP team does it when you ask, so the structure stays clean for everyone in your club.

Step by step: see the posts of a team or a group of players

  1. Open My Club and choose the Players tab.
  2. Open Section and pick one, or keep All sections. Each section shows how many players it has.
  3. Open Team and pick one, or keep All teams. If you haven't picked a section, you'll see "Choose a section to see its teams".
  4. Tap one or more players to see only their posts. Use Search a player to find someone quickly.
  5. Use the same filters as in the Content Library (format, platform, period…), save posts to a board, unlock an AI analysis or drag a post to your Content Plan.

The Club profiles, Competitors and Content creators tabs work the same way.

Step by step: ask SCP to add or change players, teams or sections

  1. Write to SCP from the contact page or to your account manager.
  2. For a new player: name, account on each network, and the team and section.
  3. For a departure or a move: the player and whether to remove them or move them to another team.
  4. For a new team or section: its name, the section it belongs to and its accounts.
  5. Once SCP has linked them, the names appear in the tabs. A name marked "Account linked, no posts imported yet" fills up as SCP uploads its posts, which happens every day.

Tip: send changes in one list at key moments (start of the season, end of the transfer window) instead of one by one.

Practical example: a football club at the start of the season

A second-division football club signs five players, loses two and creates a new U19 team in its academy. The comms manager sends SCP a single message with the five new accounts (first team), the two players to remove and the U19 squad's accounts. Once it's done, she opens Players, picks Section Football and Team First team, and taps the five new signings. In one view she sees how each of them presents themselves on social media, which gives her football social media content ideas for the welcome posts: who already films behind-the-scenes, who prefers photos, who speaks to camera.

Tips

  • Filter by team before a match to see what your players posted that week and reshare or plan around it.
  • Combine Players with the Match Day filters to find social media ideas for sports teams around a specific moment.
  • Compare your team with a rival in Competitors using the same period.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a player to My Club myself?

No. Send the player's name, accounts and team to SCP through the contact page or your account manager, and SCP links them.

Can I see several players at once?

Yes. In the Players tab, tap as many players as you want and you'll see the posts of all of them together.

Why does a player say "Account linked, no posts imported yet"?

The account is already linked, but its posts haven't been uploaded yet. SCP uploads your club's posts every day.

Can I have sections for other sports?

Yes. Sections can be the first team, the women's team, the academy or other sports; ask SCP to create them.
