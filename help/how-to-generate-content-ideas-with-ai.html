How to generate content ideas using AI

AI ToolsRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Sports Content Playbook doesn't generate ideas from a blank prompt. Instead, its AI helps you find ideas that already worked (sentence search and smart filters, Overperforming, More like this) and then turns the one you choose into your own version: the AI analysis' Ready to use block gives you 3 hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft for your club.

How SCP's AI gives you ideas

There is no box in Sports Content Playbook where you type "give me ideas for Saturday" and get a list back: no AI chat and no free-text idea generator. What SCP has is a library of more than 500,000 real posts from clubs, leagues and brands, labelled by AI, and an AI analysis that turns any of them into a plan for your club. The difference matters: every idea you get has already been published by a real account, and you can see how it performed before you spend time on it.

The workflow has three steps: describe what you need, narrow to what works, and convert one post into your version.

Step 1: describe what you need in a sentence

  1. Open the Content Library.
  2. In the search box (Search: signings, a club, reels…) type a sentence, for example transfer reels with a sponsor that worked.
  3. SCP turns your words into filters: here, Transfers, the reel format, sponsor-related filters and Overperforming. Check the active filters in the left column and adjust them.
  4. Use the suggestion chips under the search box for related searches.

This sentence search is free; it doesn't use your AI analyses. It works because SCP's AI has already labelled the posts with smart filters such as Match Day moments (every plan) and Brands & sponsors (Pro).

Step 2: keep only what works

  • Overperforming (in What works): posts that got at least 2x the median of their own account that quarter.
  • Replicable ideas: formats you can recreate with your own players, venue and footage.
  • Clubs like mine and Favs: posts from the competitions, sports and favourite profiles in your Profile.
  • More like this on any post: more versions of the same idea (same format, sport and competition). It's the fastest way to turn one good idea into several.

Step 3: let the AI write your version

  1. Open the post you like and click Unlock AI Analysis (uses 1 analysis), then View AI Analysis.
  2. Read How to adapt it to your club.
  3. Go to Ready to use: pick one of the 3 Hook ideas, follow the Shot list (3-6 shots with a tip each) and edit the Caption draft (up to 3 lines with [placeholders]).
  4. On Plus and Pro, drag the post to your Content Plan; the AI's adaptation goes into the entry's notes. Fill my week fills your empty days with posts from your favourites.

Example: a week of football ideas in 20 minutes

A football club's social media manager needs sports content ideas for a week with a Saturday home match and a new sponsor. She looks for football social media content ideas in three searches: matchday preview reels that worked, full-time result carousels and, on Pro, a search for sponsor integrations. From the first she uses More like this to get two more sports reels ideas. She analyses the two posts she'll actually make and drags all five to her Content Plan. Result: a full week of sports social media content ideas, each with a real reference and two with a hook, shot list and caption ready.

Tips

  • Search first, analyse later. Filters and sentence search are free; analyses are limited (Starter 5, Plus 10, Pro 30 per month).
  • Analyse only the posts you'll really produce. On team plans, teammates' analyses are free to read.
  • Want to use your own AI assistant too? The free resources include a Prompt Pack with 14 prompts.
  • If a club or league you follow is missing, ask SCP to add it via contact; it's included in every plan.

Among AI tools for sports content creation, SCP's approach is to start from proof, not from a blank page.

Frequently asked questions

Does SCP have an AI idea generator or chat?

No. SCP's AI labels the library with smart filters and analyses individual posts. Ideas come from real posts, and the analysis gives you hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft to make your version.

Does the sentence search use AI analyses?

No. It turns your words into filters and is free on every plan.

How many ideas can I get from one analysis?

Each analysis gives 3 hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft for one post. Use More like this to find variations of the same idea without spending analyses.

Can I generate ideas in Spanish?

Yes. Search works in Spanish, and analyses are translated to Spanish the first time someone opens them in Spanish.
