How to manage sponsors in My Club

My ClubUpdated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

Sponsors in My Club are added by the Sports Content Playbook team when you send their accounts. Once linked, the Sponsors tab shows every post from a brand or only the posts linked to your club, and the Brands & sponsors filters in the library show how other clubs activate their partners.

How sponsors work in My Club

The Sponsors tab in My Club collects the posts of the brands that sponsor your club. You can view them in two modes:

  • All the brand's posts: everything the brand posts, about the club or not.
  • Posts linked to the club: the club's posts where the sponsor is tagged and the brand's posts that mention the club.

Members can't add or remove sponsors themselves. You send the list to SCP and SCP links the accounts.

Step by step: ask SCP to add or change a sponsor

  1. Write to SCP from the contact page or to your account manager.
  2. Send the brand's name and its accounts on each network.
  3. If the sponsor belongs to one section or team only (for example the academy's kit sponsor), say so.
  4. For a sponsor whose deal has ended, ask SCP to remove it.
  5. Once linked, the brand appears in the Sponsors tab. Posts are uploaded every day.

Step by step: see your sponsors' posts

  1. Open My Club and choose the Sponsors tab.
  2. If you need to, pick a Section and a Team.
  3. Tap one or more sponsors, or use Search a sponsor.
  4. Choose All the brand's posts or Posts linked to the club. If you see "No posts from these sponsors mention the club yet.", there are no linked posts for that selection.
  5. Filter by period or format, save posts to a board in Saved boards, unlock an AI analysis or drag a post to your Content Plan.

Find sponsor ideas from other clubs

My Club includes everything in Pro, so you also have the Brands & sponsors filters in the Content Library: Business goal, Sponsors, Integration and Sponsor sector, plus Call to action and Collaborations. Use them to see how other clubs integrate partners in their posts. These filters are detected by AI on Instagram posts from October 2026, so they're especially useful for sports Instagram content ideas; older posts are still being analysed.

Practical example: a handball club before a renewal meeting

A handball club has two main sponsors: an insurance company and a local supermarket. Before the renewal meeting with the supermarket, the marketing manager opens Sponsors, taps the supermarket and chooses Posts linked to the club with the period This season. She saves the posts to a board, checks views and likes on each card and downloads the AI analysis PDF of the two that performed best. Then, in the library, she filters Sponsor sector and Integration to find what other clubs did with food retail partners, and brings three new ideas to the meeting.

Tips and limits

  • Use Posts linked to the club to review the partnership and All the brand's posts to learn the brand's tone before you pitch an idea.
  • SCP only uses public posts and doesn't connect to your social accounts, so private sponsor reports aren't included.
  • Prepare social media ideas for sports teams that your sponsor can also share, and check how they performed in the library first.

This way Sports Content Playbook works as one of your sports marketing tools and supports a sports social media strategy where sponsors have a clear place. Next: how to use the Content Planner.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a sponsor to My Club myself?

No. Send the brand's name and accounts to SCP through the contact page or your account manager, and SCP links them.

What is the difference between the two sponsor modes?

All the brand's posts shows everything the brand publishes. Posts linked to the club shows the club's posts where the sponsor is tagged and the brand's posts that mention the club.

Which plan has the Brands & sponsors filters?

Pro, and also Enterprise (My Club), because it includes everything in Pro.

Does SCP show private sponsor metrics?

No. SCP uses public posts only and doesn't connect to your social accounts, so it shows the public views and likes of each post.
