How to find your first content idea in under 5 minutes

Getting StartedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

In under 5 minutes you can go from login to a saved, adapted idea in Sports Content Playbook: search or filter the Content Library for your competition, open a post that performed well, save it to a board, unlock its AI analysis and copy the adaptation, hook template and caption draft for your club.

Before you start

You need an active plan or the 7-day free trial (the trial includes 5 AI analyses). The tutorial uses one AI analysis. Every step works on every plan except the optional last one, which needs the Content Plan (Plus or Pro).

Timing tip: the AI analysis of an image or carousel takes up to a minute; a video takes about 3 minutes because the AI watches the whole video. If you want to stay well under 5 minutes, start the analysis first and save the post while it runs.

Step by step (about 5 minutes)

  1. Log in (0:00). Use your email and password or Google. You land directly in the Content Library.
  2. Narrow the library (0:30). In the search box (Search: signings, a club, reels…) type a sentence like "post-match reels" and press Enter: Sports Content Playbook turns your words into filters for free. Then open Inspiration from and pick your Competition.
  3. Keep what worked (1:00). In What works, tick Overperforming (posts that got at least 2x their account's usual numbers) or Replicable ideas (easy to copy with your own assets). Set the period to This month.
  4. Open a post (1:30). Click a card. Play the embedded post and check Tags and caption to see its original text and hashtags.
  5. Unlock the analysis (2:00). Click Unlock AI Analysis. It uses 1 of your monthly analyses; if generation fails, it isn't counted.
  6. Save it (2:15). While the analysis runs, click Save and pick a board, or create one with New board (for example "This week").
  7. Adapt it (3:00). Click View AI Analysis and go straight to How to adapt it to your club and Ready to use. Copy the Hook template, pick one of the 3 hook ideas, check the shot list and fill the [placeholders] in the caption draft with your club's names.
  8. Plan it (4:30, Plus and Pro). Drag the post onto a day in the Content Plan panel, or use the + under the card to pick a day. Because it has an analysis, the AI's adaptation is copied into the entry's notes.

Example: a second-division football club

A social media manager at a second-division football club needs football social media content ideas for Sunday's post-match. She types "post-match reels", selects her league in Competition and ticks Overperforming. The top result is a 20-second dressing-room reel from a rival club. She unlocks the analysis, saves the reel to her "This week" board and reads the adaptation: the analysis suggests filming the same shot sequence with her own players and gives three hooks. She copies the caption draft, replaces [player] and [rival] with real names, and drags the post to Sunday. Four and a half minutes.

If you are short on time

  • Use Replicable ideas when your team has no video editor: these are sports content ideas you can recreate with your own players, venue and footage.
  • Sort by Most viewed for sports reels ideas that already proved they can reach a big audience.
  • Check How it performed in the analysis: "Above its usual" means the post really beat that account's median, not just that the account is big.
  • Fill your Favourites in Profile once; afterwards Favs and Clubs like mine skip the filtering step.

What "adapted" means here

SCP does not create the finished post for you, and there is no free-text caption generator or AI chat. "Adapted" means you leave with a concrete plan for your club: the AI's adaptation, a hook, a shot list and a caption draft ready to fill in. You still shoot and publish with your usual tools. If the analysis says Hard to replicate, go back to the results and pick another post; that is a useful answer too.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tutorial work during the free trial?

Yes. The trial gives you the full library and 5 AI analyses. Only the last step, dragging the post to the Content Plan, needs Plus or Pro.

What if I open the same analysis again later?

It's free. Reopening an analysis you already unlocked doesn't use another one; find it in My AI analyses.

Can I share the idea with a colleague?

Yes. Copy the post's Public link: anyone can open that single post without an account, and the link never expires. You can also download the analysis as a PDF.

Why did the analysis take longer than a minute?

Videos (reels, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) take about 3 minutes because the AI watches frames of the whole video and reads a transcript of what is said.
