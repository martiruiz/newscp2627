What is Sports Content Playbook and how does it work?

Getting StartedRecommendedUpdated 12 November 2026 · 2 min read

Sports Content Playbook (SCP) is the content platform for sports social media teams: a library of 500,000+ real posts from 770+ clubs and 110+ competitions, an AI analysis that explains why each post worked, and a weekly Content Plan to schedule your own versions.

What Sports Content Playbook is

Sports Content Playbook (SCP) is one of the sports social media tools built specifically for the people who run the accounts of clubs, leagues, federations, agencies and sports brands. Its promise is simple: know what you will post on Monday. You see what is working for the clubs in your league and make your own version in about 10 minutes.

Instead of scrolling dozens of accounts every morning, you open one library with thousands of real posts, already tagged and sorted, and you find sports content ideas that have been proven with real audiences.

How it works

SCP tracks the best sports accounts every day and adds new posts daily. The library holds more than 500,000 posts from 770+ clubs and 110+ competitions across 17 sports (football, basketball, handball, NFL, MotoGP, NHL, cycling, baseball, tennis and more) and five networks: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Facebook. These numbers are live and keep growing.

AI tags every post with filters (format, match-day moment, sponsor, tone and more), so you can narrow the library down to exactly the type of post you need. SCP only uses public posts: you never connect your own social media accounts, and the platform follows the GDPR.

The three tools

  1. Content Library (every plan): search and filter real posts, save them to private boards and share a public link to any single post.
  2. AI analysis (every plan): one click explains a post's hook, why it works, how it performed against the account's usual numbers and how to adapt it to your club, with ready-to-use hook ideas, a shot list and a caption draft. This is how SCP works as one of the AI tools for sports content creation.
  3. Content Plan (Plus and Pro): a sports content planning tool with week and month views where you drag posts from the library onto a day and track each idea from Idea to Published.

Clubs on the Enterprise plan also get My Club, a private space with the posts of their own players, sponsors, teams and competitors, set up together with the SCP team.

Who it is for

Sports Content Playbook is used by club social media managers, content teams, agencies, leagues, federations and sports brands. If an account your team follows is not in the library yet, you can ask SCP to add it through the contact page; this is included in every plan.

A practical example

A second-division football club has a home game on Saturday and a new kit sponsor. The social media manager opens the library, filters by Match Day and their own competition, and finds three pre-match reels from rival clubs that performed well. She runs an AI analysis on one of them, copies the hook template and the shot list, and drags the post into Friday on her Content Plan. The idea is planned before lunch.

What SCP does not do

SCP does not publish to your networks, does not connect to your accounts and does not let you download other clubs' videos or images. It shows you what works and helps you plan your own version; you create and post the content with your usual tools.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to connect my Instagram or TikTok account?

No. Sports Content Playbook only uses public posts, so you never connect your social media accounts.

Which sports and networks are covered?

17 sports, including football, basketball, handball, NFL, MotoGP, NHL, cycling, baseball and tennis, on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Facebook.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. A card is required, nothing is charged today and you can cancel during the trial without being charged.

How often is new content added?

SCP tracks the best accounts daily, so new posts are added to the library every day.
