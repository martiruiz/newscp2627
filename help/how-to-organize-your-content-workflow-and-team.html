Team workflow

How to organize your content workflow and team

Content PlannerUpdated 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

In Sports Content Playbook each person has their own account and a private Content Plan, so there is no shared team calendar. A team organises its workflow with statuses (Idea, Planned, In production, Published), a weekly base, exports of the week to Excel, PDF or .ics, shared search and post links, and AI analyses that teammates on the same plan can read for free.

How teamwork works in Sports Content Playbook

Sports Content Playbook is a sports content planning tool built around individual accounts. Paid plans are priced per user, and the owner can add up to 5 users from the billing page. Each person signs in with their own email and keeps their own Saved boards, their own Content Plan and their own monthly AI analysis allowance.

The Content Plan is private to each user: colleagues cannot open or edit your calendar. What the team does share is:

  • Exports of your week as PDF, Excel (.xlsx) or Calendar (.ics for Google, Outlook or Apple).
  • Links to searches: filters live in the URL, so copying the link of a search gives a colleague the same results.
  • Public post links: anyone can open that single post, without an account, and the link never expires.
  • AI analyses: on team plans, an analysis unlocked by one teammate is free to read for the others.

The Content Plan is included in Plus and Pro. On Starter there is no planner, so the team workflow below needs Plus or Pro.

Add your colleagues to the plan

  1. Go to Profile and open Billing & membership.
  2. In Your team, use the Users − / + buttons to add a seat (the page shows the cost before you confirm).
  3. Type the person's address in Colleague's email and click Add user.
  4. They get access when they sign in to Sports Content Playbook with that same email.
  5. Repeat up to 5 users in total. If you need more seats, request a custom plan at contact.html?topic=custom-plan.

Each new user pays the plan's per-user price and gets their own AI analysis allowance (5, 10 or 30 a month depending on the plan).

Use statuses and the weekly base as your pipeline

Every entry in the Content Plan has a status: Idea, Planned, In production and Published. Giving an idea a date turns it into Planned automatically. Treat these four steps as your production line and move each entry forward as it progresses.

  1. Open Content Plan and click New plan if you want a separate plan for a project (for example Pre-season or Women's team).
  2. Set up a Weekly base with your recurring posts (matchday graphic, training reel, sponsor post). They appear as red placeholders until you plan them.
  3. Drop undecided ideas in the No date tray and pull from Ideas & inspiration, which lists your Saved boards.
  4. For each entry, choose the type (Post, Carousel, Reel, Story, YouTube, TikTok) and fill in Caption and Notes. Changes save automatically.
  5. Click Download, choose PDF, Excel or Calendar (.ics) for This week, and tick Include “No date” ideas if the team should see them too.

Example: a three-person team at a second-division football club

The social media manager owns the Content Plan. On Monday morning she uses Fill my week in the library's Content Plan panel, swaps a few posts, and drags two analysed posts onto Wednesday and Friday, so the AI's adaptation lands in each entry's notes. She exports This week to Excel and posts the file in the team chat, and adds the .ics to the club's shared sports content calendar in Google.

The video editor receives the saved-search link “post-match reels from the league” and the public links of the two reference posts. He opens the AI analyses from My AI analyses → My team's without spending his own allowance and follows the Shot list. The designer does the same with a carousel reference.

As pieces move forward, the manager updates each status to In production and then Published, and re-exports on Thursday for the weekend. This is a simple sports social media strategy routine: one owner of the calendar, everyone else working from shared links.

Limits and tips

  • There is no shared or co-edited calendar, no task assignment and no comments inside the Content Plan. Agree on one person who owns the plan and shares exports.
  • Saved boards are private too. To share a set of posts, send the link of the search or the public link of each post.
  • Each user's AI allowance is separate. Check My team's before unlocking a post, because a colleague may already have analysed it.
  • If a teammate needs posts for their own plan, they can build it from the same shared search: like most sports social media tools, Sports Content Playbook keeps each planner personal.

Frequently asked questions

Can my whole team edit the same Content Plan?

No. Each Content Plan is private to the user who created it. Share it by exporting the week as PDF, Excel or .ics, and share searches and posts through their links.

Do my teammates' AI analyses use my allowance?

No. On team plans an analysis unlocked by a colleague is free for you to read and shows who analysed it. Each user has their own monthly allowance.

How many people can I add to my plan?

Up to 5 users from Billing & membership, each paying the per-user price. For more seats, request a custom plan through the contact page.

Can someone without an account see a post I want to copy?

Yes. Use the Public link button on the post card: anyone can open that single post, no account needed, and the link never expires.
