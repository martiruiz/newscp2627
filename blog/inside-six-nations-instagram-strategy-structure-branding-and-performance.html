Inside Six Nations’ Instagram strategy: structure, branding and performance

By Josep · 19 March 2026 · 1 min read

The Instagram profile of the Six Nations presents an interesting case within social media in sports, combining a dynamic publishing approach with a strong visual identity. One of the first elements that stands out is the lack of pinned posts. This creates a more “volatile” feed, where the top content is constantly changing depending on the latest matchdays or narratives. While this reduces long-term visibility of key assets, it reinforces a real-time, competition-driven storytelling approach.

From a content perspective, the account leans heavily on a clear and repetitive structure. During the tournament, communication was largely built around match results, highlights (both video and photo), and key moments from games. Post-competition, the focus has shifted toward recap content – summarising performances and reinforcing the tournament’s biggest stories.

Visually, the use of orange as a dominant color is a defining feature. Tied to the competition’s branding and Guinness sponsorship, this color creates immediate recognition across all formats. Whether through graphics, scorecards, or branded assets, this consistency strengthens identity within the crowded landscape of social media in sports.
