Winning loud, losing smart: inside Super Bowl Instagram tactics

By Josep · 11 February 2026 · 1 min read

This year’s Super Bowl ended with the Seattle Seahawks defeating the New England Patriots 29–13, in a spectacle also marked by Bad Bunny’s halftime performance. But beyond the scoreboard, the real storytelling battle unfolded on Instagram – a perfect case study for anyone working in social media in sports and analyzing strategies inside Sports Content Playbook.

Seattle Seahawks: turning victory into a visual takeover

As champions, the Seahawks transformed their Instagram into a celebration hub. The “Champions” design was not only posted – it became their profile picture, another Champions design was pinned to the feed, and it was highlighted in Stories. This level of visual consistency reinforces brand memory and is something content creators in the sports industry should note.

Post-game, Seattle amplified the moment with trophy photos, locker-room celebrations, MVP content, Disneyland appearances, AI-generated champion visuals, Gatorade collaborations, and a steady flow of highlights. The volume and variety show a clear understanding of momentum management in social media in sports. Winning content wasn’t just posted – it was extended, diversified, and monetized.

New England Patriots: controlled and composed

The Patriots took a more restrained approach. Pre-game energy included motivational content featuring Bon Jovi, tunnel walk visuals, and rallying messages. During the match, they maintained optimism with highlight snippets and encouragement posts.
