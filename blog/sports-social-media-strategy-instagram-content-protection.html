Sports social media strategy: turn copied Reels into an audience with Instagram's Content protection

By Martí Ruiz · 18 November 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Instagram's Content protection finds copies of your Reels and lets you block them or tag ‘original by’.
  • The ‘original by’ option can send the copy's audience back to your account.
  • Set a simple rule for block, tag or allow, and agree the allow list with your sponsors.
  • Make your originals recognisable with a consistent opening frame, a club name and a named series.

Clubs have watched their goals appear on other accounts for years. This week Instagram gave them a way to react. According to TechCrunch and a post from the @creators account, Instagram has launched Content protection, a tool that scans the platform for copies of your Reels and alerts you when it finds one. For any sports social media strategy, the interesting part is not the block button. It is what you can do with the copy.

What Instagram launched

The tool already existed on Facebook. Now it reaches Instagram. It looks for copies of your Reels across the platform. When it finds one, it notifies you and you choose what happens next.

  • Block the copy on Instagram and on Facebook.
  • Leave it up and add ‘original by’, with a link to your own video.

Instagram also plans to test detection of AI-generated videos that reuse your audio. And if a sponsor publishes your clips, you can put that account on an allow list so it is not flagged.

Sports social media strategy: block the copy or borrow its audience

The second option is the strategic one. A copy that stays online with an ‘original by’ link stops taking audience from you. It sends viewers to your account instead.

This is a useful way to read the news. The accounts that stand out in our daily analysis do not treat reach as something to guard. They treat it as something to route. A fan who sees your goal on a fan page is already interested. The link gives that fan a next step.

A simple rule can help your team decide:

  • Block copies that remove your branding, misuse your audio or hurt a partner.
  • Tag copies from fan pages and media that celebrate the moment.
  • Allow your own partners and players' accounts that post your clips.

Sponsors and the allow list

The allow list matters for sports. Sponsors, leagues and athletes often repost club content on purpose. Without a list, those posts could be flagged like any other copy. Before you switch the tool on, collect the accounts that have permission and agree the rules with your commercial team. The same document can then feed your sports marketing tools reports, so the club can show which shared posts brought traffic back.

Ten sports content ideas that make your original easy to find

Protection works better when the original is clearly yours. These sports content ideas make that easier. They are also practical sports social media content ideas for a normal week.

  1. A recognisable opening frame. Same colours and first second in every Reel.
  2. A spoken or on-screen club name. Hard to crop out.
  3. A short series title. Fans search for a named format.
  4. A reply Reel. Answer a popular copy with your own angle.
  5. The full version. Post the extended cut and link to it from copies.
  6. The behind-the-camera shot. The copy shows the goal, you show the bench.
  7. A pinned comment. Point viewers to the series or playlist.
  8. A Stories follow-up. Ask fans which moment to unpack next.
  9. A player voice-over. Hard to reproduce, easy to recognise.
  10. A season recap. Gather the most copied moments into one piece.

For football social media content ideas, goals and celebrations are the most copied moments, so add a branded layer. Teams building sports Instagram content ideas can test the ‘original by’ route on one format first. sports TikTok content ideas and sports reels ideas follow the same logic: protect the version that carries your identity.

Takeaway

Content protection is not only a defence tool. It is one of the sports social media trends to watch: platforms now let you turn a copy into a path back to your account. These social media ideas for sports teams start with a clear rule, a short allow list and an original that is easy to recognise.
