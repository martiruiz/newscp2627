Sports social media trends: why Haaland won an Instagram Ring

By Martí Ruiz · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • The Instagram Rings reward creativity and bold ideas, not follower counts.
  • Haaland grew by mixing football, humour and participation, including a comment that came close to 3 million likes.
  • One player can concentrate most of a team's reach, so the personal account deserves its own plan.
  • Reserve one low-production piece a week and define the tone you want to keep.
  • A visible change on your profile can become news if you prepare the explanation.

Open Erling Haaland's Instagram profile today and something looks different: a blue background and a ring around his photo. It is not a paid feature or a verification change. It is a recognition from the platform itself. Haaland is one of the 25 winners of the 2026 Instagram Rings, and for anyone following sports social media trends, the reason behind the award matters more than the badge.

This article explains what the Rings are, why Haaland won and which lessons a club, federation or sports brand can apply to its own sports social media strategy.

What happened

At the end of October, Instagram announced the winners of the 2026 Rings, the second edition of an award for creators who shaped the conversation on and off the platform. There are 25 winners worldwide. Besides Haaland, the list includes Subway Takes creator Kareem Rahma, the Mexican band Latin Mafia, Brazilian singer-songwriter Joyce Alane and Indian model Bhavitha Mandave.

A jury from fashion, music, film and sport chose them, with names such as Donatella Versace, A$AP Rocky, Chloé Zhao and Mikaela Shiffrin, plus Instagram head Adam Mosseri. Winners get a physical ring, a digital ring around the profile photo and profile customisation, such as the blue background Haaland now has. Their work is also shown on screens in Times Square and on the facade of the Dream Hotel in New York.

What the Rings reward, and what they do not

They do not reward followers or reach. The criteria are creativity, creative courage and bold ideas. Fox Sports México sums it up as recognising people who use Instagram in an authentic and creative way, beyond their popularity. Vivan Odior, who leads marketing for Meta's consumer apps, says creativity is not only what you post but all the work behind it. In Haaland's case, Instagram highlighted his mix of football, humour and a personality of his own.

Why Haaland: the World Cup as a springboard

The summer numbers explain the award. According to Social Blade, Haaland went from 40.7 to about 70 million Instagram followers during the 2026 World Cup. Lefty, which measured the period from 1 June to 16 July, counts 29.6 million new followers, more than any other player at the tournament.

Also according to Lefty, across Instagram and TikTok he published 187 pieces that generated more than 3.6 billion impressions, and he alone accounted for 94% of the social reach of the whole Norwegian team.

Goals helped: seven in the tournament and Norway's first World Cup quarter-finals. But the growth came mostly from how he moved on the platform. He rode the wave of memes and AI-generated fan videos. Before Norway against Brazil, he commented on an AI video in which he appeared singing with Vinícius Jr. By his own account, that comment came close to 3 million likes, one of the most liked in Instagram history. The record, the source notes, still belongs to Cristiano Ronaldo.

Five lessons for clubs and sports marketing teams

1. Less volume, more idea

If the platform rewards creative courage and not frequency, posting more is not the answer to a profile that does not grow. Good sports content ideas beat a fuller calendar. What to do: take your last 10 posts and write the idea of each in one sentence. If you cannot, the problem is not the schedule. It is the idea. Use these sports social media content ideas as a filter before anything goes live.

2. Comments are content too

One of the biggest moments of Haaland's summer was not a post of his own but a comment. What to do: set one weekly participation action from the club account, such as answering fans, reacting to a rival's meme or commenting on league content. It is one of the simplest social media ideas for sports teams, and it also feeds your sports reels ideas, because the best replies can become short videos.

3. The player is the channel

When one player concentrates 94% of a national team's reach, the personal account weighs more than the institutional one. What to do: agree with one or two players on a content frame of their own: what the club publishes, what they create together and what stays free. Then measure the reach their accounts generate. It is a strong base for football social media content ideas.

4. Personality before polish

Coverage of his growth agrees on one thing: there was no manufactured communication, only a natural and close presence. What to do: reserve one piece a week with low production, such as backstage, inside humour or reactions. Natural can also be designed: define the tone and keep it. This works for sports Instagram content ideas and for sports TikTok content ideas alike.

5. A visual signal can be news

The colour change on Haaland's profile produced explainers such as "why is his Instagram blue?" in Mexican and Honduran media, and the award was also covered in places such as India and Germany. What to do: when you change something visible on your profile or content, such as a new format, a redesign or a recognition, prepare the explanation and let curiosity work for you. Put these moments in your sports content calendar so they are planned, not improvised.

What you can do this week

  • Write the idea of your last three posts in one sentence.
  • Analyse five sports accounts that grew because of a format, not a signing, and note their hook and structure.
  • Define one weekly participation action in the comments.
  • Talk to a player about content of their own.

The Rings reward ideas with personality, not follower counts. Haaland's growth came from a natural voice, humour and participation, not from polished communication. Pick one of the five lessons and apply it to your next week of content.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Instagram Rings?

Awards from Instagram (Meta) that recognise 25 creators for their creativity and their ability to shape culture. 2026 is the second edition. Winners receive a physical ring and digital badges on their profile.

Why is Haaland's Instagram blue?

Because he won a Ring. The blue background and the ring around the photo are part of the recognition, not a paid feature or a change in verification.

How many Instagram followers does Haaland have?

According to Social Blade, he went from about 40.7 million to about 70 million followers during the 2026 World Cup.

How are the winners chosen?

A jury from fashion, music, film and sport nominates and votes. Follower count is not a criterion.
