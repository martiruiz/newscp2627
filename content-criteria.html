Content Selection Criteria

At Sports Content Playbook (SCP), we have a multidisciplinary team of professionals specialized in sports digital marketing, content creation, and trend analysis. Our mission is to identify, analyze, and select the best sports content published around the world to incorporate it into the platform and make it available to industry professionals.

Selection Framework: The 10 Excellence Criteria

Every piece of content we incorporate into SCP goes through a rigorous validation process based on 10 fundamental criteria:

1. Demonstrable Impact and Performance

We value content with measurable and verifiable results:

  • High engagement: likes, views, comments, shares, and saves
  • Audience growth: increase in followers or impressions
  • Impact: virality, media mentions, or resonance within the industry

Why it matters: Performance is evidence that content connects with the audience and generates real value.

2. Originality and Creativity

We seek pieces that surprise and inspire:

  • Innovative ideas that break from the ordinary
  • Disruptive or unusual formats in the sports field
  • Creative executions that serve as references for other creators

Why it matters: Creativity is what differentiates good content from memorable content.

3. Technical Quality and Visual Clarity

We only select well-produced content:

  • Careful and professional visual composition
  • Polished editing: rhythm, transitions, color, and sound
  • Clear narrative that facilitates message comprehension

Why it matters: Technical quality conveys professionalism and credibility.

4. Alignment with Global Trends

The team constantly analyzes what works at the highest level:

  • Growing formats at elite clubs (Real Madrid, NBA, PSG, NFL…)
  • Platform trends: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
  • New sports content consumption behaviors

Why it matters: Staying up-to-date with trends allows you to anticipate and lead, not just follow.

5. Strategic Value and Applicability

We prioritize content that provides real solutions:

  • Replicability: ideas that can be adapted without losing their essence
  • Opportunity threads: content for key moments (post-match, signings, anniversaries, club day…)
  • Proven best practices in professional environments

Why it matters: Content must be useful, not just inspiring.

6. Differentiation by Sport and Context

Each discipline has its own codes:

  • We analyze references by sport (football, basketball, American football, handball, tennis, rugby, water polo…)
  • We value the adaptability of the idea to different contexts and competitions
  • We respect the narrative particularities of each sport

Why it matters: Effective content understands and respects the visual and cultural language of its sport.

7. Temporal Relevance

Timing is key:

  • Recent content aligned with current events
  • Season moments: pre-season, derbies, finals, kit launches
  • Annual opportunities: transfer market, commemorations, awards

Why it matters: Sports content lives at the moment. Capturing it when it’s relevant multiplies its impact.

8. Replicability and Ease of Execution

One of our pillars:

  • The piece must have a clear and transferable idea
  • It must be applicable with diverse resources (not just for big clubs)
  • It must include elements that facilitate its adaptation

Why it matters: We want any team, big or small, to be able to find inspiration and execute.

9. Consistency with Professional Values

We exclude content that:

  • Conveys negative or unethical values
  • Uses sensationalist or toxic narratives
  • Does not respect the dignity of athletes or fans

We promote:

  • Good narratives that elevate sport
  • Respectful and inclusive communication
  • Values aligned with industry professionalism

Why it matters: Sports content has social responsibility. We must be consistent with the values we want to project.

10. Internal Quality Validation

Quality assurance process:

  • Double review by the editorial team
  • Verification of compliance with all criteria
  • Immediate rejection of dubious or inconsistent pieces

Why it matters: The consistency and excellence of SCP depend on rigorous and constant filtering.

Our Commitment

Every piece of content you’ll find at Sports Content Playbook has been selected to help you:

  • Work faster with proven quality references
  • Gain confidence in your creative decisions
  • Be inspired by the best examples in the world
  • Apply proven ideas that work

Contact and Continuous Improvement

These criteria are constantly evolving. If you have suggestions, use cases, or want to propose content, contact us at support@sportscontentplaybook.com. The community is a fundamental part of SCP.
