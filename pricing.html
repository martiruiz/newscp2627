Trial, billing, users and AI analyses, explained. Anything else? Write to us or book a 30-minute demo, right here.

You get 7 days free with full access to your plan. We ask for a card to start, but nothing is charged until the trial ends, and if you cancel before then you pay nothing. On monthly plans the first charge covers only the days until the 1st of the month; after that you're billed on the 1st.

Yes, from Account → Billing in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid and you won't be charged again. Your saved boards and content plans stay in your account if you come back.

Yes, whenever you want. Upgrades and new users apply straight away and you only pay the difference for the rest of the period; downgrades apply at the end of the period you've paid. You'll always see the exact amount before confirming. Yearly plans give you 2 months free.

Prices are in euros, VAT included; if your card is in another currency, your bank converts it. Monthly plans renew on the 1st of each month and yearly plans on the same date each year. Every invoice is in Account → Billing, and companies can add their VAT number at checkout.

Your analyses renew every month. If you need more before then, you can buy an extra pack: it's a one-time payment and the analyses never expire. Opening an analysis you've already unlocked is always free.

Every plan works for up to 5 users, and each user can sign in on up to two devices. For more users, or for a club or agency with several teams, we prepare a custom plan.
